PSCD Year 5     -       Level 6 
Subject Focus: Intrapersonal and Interpersonal awareness 
I can prepare a set of rules for the group. 
I can talk about the importance of rules for the effective functioning of the group.  
I can make a  list of  the  consequences when breaking the ground rules established by the group. 
I can list positive qualities that I have and list qualities I need to improve upon. 
I can talk about myself to my peers and teachers. 
I can identify and talk about different emotions that arise in different situations. 
I can express my emotions. 
I can talk about the importance of understanding the emotions of others. 
I can show how I can regulate and manage my emotions. 
I can describe the characteristics of a good friend. 
I can identify healthy ways of how friends can express their feelings with each other. 
I  can explain why caring, sharing  and love are important for  building positive friendships. 
I can explain what is a bully and how to identify one. 
I can describe the negative effects of the different types of bullying on the victim. 
I can show how to avoid bullies, help others when being bullied  and where to seek assistance if necessary.  
I can define what is assertive, passive and aggressive behaviour. 
I can illustrate how to be assertive by writing or saying ‘I’ messages. 
I can identify that every child has rights and understand why there is a need for children’s rights. 
I can talk about why some children do not enjoy the same rights.    
I can describe why rights carry responsibilities. 
I can talk about the causes that cause people to leave their countries. 
I can explain the meaning of asylum seekers, migrants and refugees.

I can identify different ways of how to help new students that come to my school or class. 
I can explain what gratitude is.

Subject Focus:  Career Exploration and Planning 
I can list the advantages and disadvantages of different jobs and careers carried out by people in my family. 
I can make a list of the skills needed for at least one job or career that work in school or contribute in some 
way to the smooth running of the school (eg. Bus driver, cleaner, librarian). 
I can give examples of stereotypical and non- stereotypical roles within the family. 
I can list the different occupations within the community I live in and discuss if gender influences who does 
which job or not. 
I can describe why  all occupations are important to the community I live in. 
I can talk about  the things I can do to contribute to my home, school community and/or   my town or village. 
I can talk about health and safety issues in various occupations and list precautions one can take to protect 
self and others.

I can discuss how  the choices I make  affect my health and well being . 
(For example, food, sleep, activities) 
I can explain clearly and in detail why and how  I need to take more  responsibility for  my own physical health 
and personal hygiene. 

I can describe how my family, friends and the media might influence me in the  choices I make in relation to 
my health and well being. 

I can identify responsible and risky choices and behaviours taking place on social networking profiles. 

I can explain what a digital footprint is.  
I can talk on how I will manage my digital footprint.  

I can describe  ways by which I will be respectful, responsible and safe when on-line and off-line. 
I can discuss  the different characterisitcs that make someone a good online and offline citizen.  
I can describe the similarities and differences between bullying face-to-face and cyberbullying. 
I can talk about different ways to tackle cyberbullying, where to seek help and how to assist someone who is 
being bullied online. 
I can talk and describe what love is and how you can show love to others.  
I can identify the changes that occur during puberty. 
I can describe why it is important to use the right language when talking about different aspects of puberty 
(e.g. private body parts). 
I can describe why everyone’s experience during puberty is different and that it begins and ends at different 
times for different people.  
I can describe how feelings and moods affect one’s  behaviour during puberty. 
I can explain how media messages affect the way one looks at  gender and  at one’s body image.    
I can talk about gender stereotypes and the different ways I can challenge them.
