Subject Focus:  Career Exploration and Planning 
I can name the different  occupations within  my family 
I can name the different occupations in the school 
I can talk about the importance of one job/career for the community 
I can discuss and challenge  what girls and boys are expected to do 
I can talk positively about what I  would like to do 
I can make a list of things of how I could contribute at home 

Subject Focus: Health, Safety and Well Being 
I can show others what I do to keep myself physically healthy 
I can explain the  need for personal hygiene e.g. getting washed and brushing teeth 
I can describe different sort of risks at home, at school and outside 
I can recognize the influence that different types of communication can have on our 
daily life 
I can recognize  and make safe choices based on right and wrong, good or bad e.g. road 
safety, sea safety and internet safety 
I  can recognize ways in which my choices and behaviours affect me and others both 
online and offline 
I can explain the three golden rules of the internet 
I can show how  I can hurt others online and offline 
I can identify the good use of media (such as education, games…)
