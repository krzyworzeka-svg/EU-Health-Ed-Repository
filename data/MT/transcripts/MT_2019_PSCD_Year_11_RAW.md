YEAR 11                                                                                                                                                   LEVEL 9 
Subject Focus:  Intrapersonal and Interpersonal awareness   
I can introduce myself to my co-students and teachers. 

I can contribute to a set of ground rules for the group. 

I can talk about the importance of ground rules for effective group functioning. 

I can talk about my values and beliefs. 

I can explain how my values and beliefs influence my choices when making decisions. 

I can talk about how my decisions effect my life and the life of those around me. 

I can discuss how volunteering and active citizenship contribute to the wellbeing of society. 

I can list the things I can do to be a proactive citizen. 

I can identify the steps involved in blood and organ donation. 

I can describe how it might feel to be part of the donation process. 

I can talk about the importance of donation for individuals and for the wellbeing of society. 

I can explain the process and the importance of voting. 

I can discuss misconceptions related to voting.  

I can distinguish between fostering and adoption. 

I can describe how fostering and adoption contribute positively to the wellbeing of the individual, the family and 
the community.

I can identify different views about abortion. 

I can talk about the various services offered to support  teenage or young parents. 

I can identify the challenges and opportunities faced during transitions.

Subject Focus: Career Exploration and Planning 
I can reflect and identify my personal characteristics.  

I can match my personal characteristics to varied jobs and careers. 

I can list the various post-secondary institutions available. 

I can identify the advantages and disadvantages of different work alternatives. 

I can recognise the important employability skills needed for different jobs and careers. 

I can name the different rights, duties and responsibilities of employers and employees. 

I can identify the varied employment support services available. 

Subject Focus: Health, Safety  and Well being 
I can recognise that everyone has a state of mental health, with ups and downs. 

I can talk about the stigma surrounding mental health and discuss how this can make a person affected by mental 
health problems feel worse. 
I can identify signs and symptoms of some mental health problems.

I can identify strategies of how to prevent and maintain a healthy mental state of mind. 

I can recognize how I can assist someone who has mental health issues. 

I can refer to the varied mental support services available to self and others. 

I can identify the rights and responsibilities in a relationship. 

I can recognise and talk about what is acceptable in an intimate and sexual relationship. 

I can explain why communication is essential for a healthy intimate and sexual relationship. 

I can distinguish between responsible and irresponsible actions in relationships. 

I can identify the risks encountered online. 

I can discuss responsible actions I can make to avoid adverse consequences online. 

I can recognise and identify different sexual orientations. 

I can recognise the importance of feeling comfortable with one’s sexual orientation. 

I can identify how alcohol or other drugs can lead to impaired decision making. 

I can identify strategies of avoiding undesired consequences under the influence of alcohol. 

I can talk about different ways bystanders can protect others against assault involving alcohol or drugs. 

I can explain what constitutes pornography  

I can recognize the possible impact of pornography on the expectations people might have on intimate and sexual 
relationships.  
I can explain the ongoing responsibility I have for staying sexually healthy. 

I can describe different sexually healthy actions and choices I can take.
