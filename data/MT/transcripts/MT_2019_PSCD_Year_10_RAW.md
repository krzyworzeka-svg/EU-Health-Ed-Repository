PSCD - YEAR 10                                                                                                                                         LEVEL 8 
Subject Focus:  Intrapersonal and  Interpersonal awareness   
I can introduce myself to my co-students and teachers. 

I can contribute to a set of ground rules for the group. 

I can talk about  the importance of ground rules for effective group functioning. 

I can talk about the importance of technology in our society. 

I can identify various uses of technology in my daily routines. 

I can explain how technology can help to improve my activities, services and life in general. 

I can communicate well through a well-versed email. 

I can recognize the importance of being respectful when communicating through digital means. 

I can identify sexist hate speech and the consequences it has on the people targeted.  

I can recognize the importance of being responsible when posting a photograph online. 

I can distinguish between what is safe and not safe when posting online. 

I can talk about the consequences and potential negative risks of personal photos becoming public. 

I can talk about what constitutes a good and positive relationship. 

I can express what I like and what I dislike in a relationship. 

I can talk about what I expect in a relationship.

I can identify the qualities that I have and what I would like to find in a partner. 

I can recognise the various stages when dating a partner. 

I can distinguish between the main types of abuse: physical, emotional, psychological and sexual. 

I can identify early warning signs of an abusive relationship and discuss why abusive behaviour is never acceptable 
in any relationship. 
I can discuss how and when I need to leave an abusive or violent relationship.   

I can identify the importance of giving consent freely and willingly without coercion and pressure. 

I can talk about the importance of consent for any intimate or/and sexual act in any type of relationship. 

I can identify the societal expectations in relationships. 

I can recognise gender stereotypes projected through the media. 

I can discuss how gender stereotyping leads to unfair expectations. 

Subject Focus: Career Exploration and Planning 
I can identify and practice the skills needed to address an audience. 

I can prepare and present myself for an interview. 

I can write a good covering letter. 

I can develop a suitable Curriculum Vitae. 

I can compile an effective portfolio. 

I can identify and demonstrate ethical behaviour in the place of work. 

 
 
 
 
 
Subject Focus: Health, Safety  and Well being 
I can recall my knowledge about aspects in growing up. 

I can identify how one’s values are shaped. 

I can recognise my personal values and how they influence my sexual choices. 

I can talk about sex and reproduction. 

I can explain what pregnancy entails. 

I can recognise the types, causes and symptoms of STIs. 

I can understand how attitudes and values influence the transmission of STIs. 

I can talk about the right way of how to wear a condom. 

I can identify and address misconceptions about condom use. 

I can talk about the facts and myths of substance abuse. 

I can recognise that legal drugs can still be harmful. 

I can recognise the level of risk related to different substances. 

I can explain how to adopt appropriate strategies to reduce risky and harmful behaviours. 

I can distinguish between wants and needs. 

I can list my needs. 

I can recognise  how advertisements influence the consumers’ choices. 

I can give examples on how to manage my finances appropriately.
