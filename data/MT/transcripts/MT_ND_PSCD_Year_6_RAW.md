PSCD YEAR 6 – LEVEL 5 
Subject Focus: Intrapersonal and Interpersonal awareness 
I can suggest ideas to help the group to formulate the ground rules.  
I can list the consequences for self and others if ground rules are not 
obeyed. 
I can talk about my personal qualities and talents and list one or more  
personal qualities I want to improve on.  
I can discuss what can affect wellbeing and resilience (e.g. life changes, 
change of school, exams, relationships and disappointments). 
I can explain how the media and social media impacts the way people think 
about themselves and express themselves (eg. body image, physical and  
mental health). 
I can discuss why people might feel differently about the same situation or 
circumstance.  
I can explain how our feelings affect the way we behave and how our 
behaviour affects others’ feelings (eg.  death of a pet) 
I can talk about empathy  and why this helps relationships grow. 
I can describe what is a conflict, disagreement or argument and why it 
happens.  
I can explain why conflicts, disagreements or arguments bring out a lot of 
intense emotions in people. 
I can discuss the skills I need to be able to listen attentively to different 
perspectives and viewpoints. 
I can list the 6 steps I need to adopt when I quarrel or argue with my 
caregivers, siblings and friends.  
I can describe the difference between bullying and conflict. 
I can propose simple strategies to help build resilience to negative opinions, 
judgments and comments.   
I can describe the different types of bullying, its negative effects and why it 
is always wrong.  
I can explain the difference between acting assertively, passively or 
aggressively and list the advantages of being assertive  
I can state where I can get support, if necessary, in different situations and 
circumstances (eg.  a trusted adult at home, a guidance or PSCD teacher). 
I can list the steps necessary to make a good decision (choosing friends/time 
management/consumer decisions/environmental decisions). 
I can list the rights and duties I have, at home, school and within the 
community.

I can talk about voting, why is it important in a democratic society and how 
voting takes place. 
I can explain why student school councils are important.   
I can identify the different skills (eg. leadership, communication, teamwork, 
organization and public speaking) that student school council foster in

I can explain what prejudice is and list down ways of how I can include 
and/or show respect to others (elderly, fellow students, relatives, foreigners,

Subject Focus: Career Exploration and Planning 
I can identify my personal learning styles and how to put them into 
practices.  
I can identify the skills required when choosing a new subject.  
I can explain what and who influences my attitudes and values in relation to 
learning, work and equality of opportunity. 
I can discuss stereotyped and misrepresented images of people, careers and 
work. 
I can explain why different types and patterns of work and careers 
(employment, self-employment, voluntary work and other types of 
employment) are important for the person and the community at large. 
I can make a list of the things I can do to contribute to my home, school 
community, town or village, country and specific places or groups I frequent. 
Subject Focus: Health, Safety and Well Being 
I can discuss how the choices I make affect my health and well-being (For 
example diet, sleep, activities, time management). 
I can explain why it is important that I start taking increased responsibility 
for my  own physical health(eg. personal hygiene, sun safety, self 
examinations and others). 
I can talk about the importance of, and strategies for, maintaining a balance 
between school, leisure, exercise, and online activities. 
I can list who, what and how I might be influenced in the choices I make in 
relation to my health and well-being.   
I can describe the concept of privacy and safety in both an offline and online 
world.  
I can show what information I should give or not give to online persons 
which I don’t know. 
I am able to talk about ways on how to protect myself online. 
I  can  talk  about  the  consequences  of  playing  games  which  are  not  age 
appropriate.

I can  recognize the different ratings of games (PEGI) and their significance. 
I can talk about the different ways of dealing with online and offline peer 
pressure.   
I can distinguish between reliable and unreliable sites/information online. 
I can indicate what different online domains refer to. 
I can list down the dangers of smoking tobacco or/and vape.  
I can discuss what I and  others can do to show they love or care for 
someone. 
I can speak about different kinds of families. 
I can  explain the physical and sexual changes taking place in my 
body.   
I can discuss why changes in the body affect one’s moods and feelings. 
I can describe the similarities and differences between different sexes. 
I can talk about the effect of the media on our perception of our physical 
image. 
I can explain why different people are attracted to same sex people or 
opposite sex people. 
I can distinguish between healthy and unhealthy food. 
I discuss who and what influences my  decisions about eating a balanced 
diet and strategies I can adopt to manage eating choices.   
I can recognize the different stages of pregnancy and inception.  
I can explain the different stages of the development of the embryo. 
I can talk why it is important for both parents or/and caregivers to love, care 
and take responsibility of a new baby.  
I can give good solutions and actions that can be taken when discussing 
situations in which  one feels uncomfortable or unsafe. 
I can speak about different types of risks in different situations and places 
and list strategies to take care of myself and others in such situations. 
I recognize the importance of the Global Goals for Sustainable Development, 
in particular Climate Action, Life below Water, Life on Land and Peace, 
Justice and Strong Institutions. 
I can discuss ways of how I can be a responsible consumer. 
I can talk about the rights of animals and the responsibilities of pet owners, 
farmers, fishermen and the general public. 
I can speak about threats to domestic and wild animals, locally and abroad, 
and what one can do to help animals. 
I can list the positive and negative aspects of change and transition to a new 
school and can list ideas of how to adjust to a new learning environment.
