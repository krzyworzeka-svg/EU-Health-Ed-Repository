Subject Focus: Intrapersonal and Interpersonal awareness   

I can assertively describe things I am good at and can express positive qualities about 
myself 
I can recognize my weaknesses and how to cope with them 
I can contribute and agree on a set of rules for the group 
I can mention things that can be done when someone breaks the ground rules of the 
group 
  I can show the importance of respect by listening to what other students  have to say 
I can  work  collaboratively  in the group 
I can  recognise and respond appropriately to a wider range of feelings in myself and 
others 
I understand and express how my feelings and reactions can change depending upon 
what is happening within and around me. 
I  recognize ways in which my own choices and behaviour affect others 
I  recognize that caring, sharing, fairness and love are important in building positive 
relationships and friendships 
I can communicate an opinion as to why bullying is unacceptable and hurtful 
I can list different strategies of how to  resolve disputes and conflicts and where to 
seek help if needed 
I can describe similarities and differences between people e.g.  gender, appearance, 
abilities, families, cultural backgrounds  
I value basic human rights shared by all peoples and all societies and that children have 
their own special rights set out in the United Nations Declaration of the Rights of the 
Child  

I can work with the other students to commemorate important dates (e.g. 
Remembrance Day) 
I can list what one can do to help immigrants and be good citizens 
I can list reasons why people go to live in another country 
I can help the school through participating in the school council or through 
volunteering 

Subject Focus:  Career Exploration and Planning 
I can talk about the different jobs and careers carried out by people in my family 
I can make a list of the skills needed for one job or career of one of my family member 
I can discuss stereotypical and non- stereotypical roles within the family 
I can list the different occupations within the community  I live in 
I can discuss about how different occupations are important to the community I live in 
I can make a list of the things I can do to contribute to my community 
I can talk about health and safety issues in various occupations.
