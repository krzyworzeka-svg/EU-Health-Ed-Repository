PSCD 
YEAR 9                                                                                                                                                     LEVEL 8 
Subject Focus:  Intrapersonal and  Interpersonal awareness   
I can introduce myself to my co-students and teachers. 
I can contribute to a set of ground rules for the group. 
I can talk about  the importance of ground rules for effective group functioning. 
I can  identify,  describe  and share different emotions. 
I can explain or describe how I deal with different emotions. 
I can recognize how emotions can effect my behaviour and how my behaviour effects others.  
I can talk about anger and the fact that I cannot control everything that happens around me. 
I am able to show that I understand what triggers my anger and the importance of expressing this emotion 
appropriately in the way I behave. 
I am able to discuss and practice different strategies on how to deal with my anger and not behave in ways that 
would have negative consequences for me and for others.  
I can recognise the initial signs and symptoms of stress.  
I can compare different stressful situations and indicate the ones which myself and others might find most 
stressful. 
I am able to discuss and practice different strategies on how to deal and reduce stress. 
I can explain what is meant by a healthy relationship and discuss its importance with others. 
I can identify different types of relationships and the skills needed to develop effective relationships. 
I can distinguish the features of a caring, non-caring and harmful relationship. 
I can talk about positive relationships, role models and personal support systems. 
I am able to discuss different strategies on how to protect myself from non-caring and harmful relationships. 
I can show that I have gained an understanding of stereotyping and how it impacts upon a variety of communities. 
I can debate whether ideas and opinions about stereotyping are factual or not.

I can argue how the different types of media can influence my perception and those of others. 
I can recognize the nature of conflict and its impact on interpersonal relationships. 
I can demonstrate the importance of communication in generating productive conflict outcomes.  
I can talk about the different conflict intervention strategies. 
Subject Focus: Career Exploration and Planning 
I can list some characteristics and skills that describe me best. 
I can talk about different skills needed for different jobs or careers. 
I can identify jobs or careers that best match my interests or skills. 
I can mention a number of reasons why people work. 
I can identify and discuss worker’s rights. 
I can identify and discuss worker’s duties. 
Subject Focus: Health, Safety  and Well being 
I can articulate and distinguish between healthy and unhealthy actions and habits. 
I can talk about my responsibilities to lead a physically and psychological   active and healthy life. 
 (e.g. self-regulation relative to practising daily health routines for cleanliness, rest, healthy eating, good posture, 
taking a hobby, time on digital games or computer and others). 
I can recognize the difference between an energy drink and a sports drink. 
I can analyse the contents of an energy drink and a sports drink and discuss the consequences (if any) of such 
drinks.  
I can describe what is meant by digital footprint. 
I can discuss how to manage my digital footprint. 
I can explain how I can start developing a positive digital footprint for future life, study and work. 
 I can talk about the risks associated with giving out personal information. 
I can identify inappropriate content, contact and conduct online. 
I can explain how I can be positive in terms of content, contact and conduct online.

I can talk about the emotional, physical and psychological changes faced during puberty.   
I can name the different body parts and their function. 
I can list and describe the components of personal hygiene.    
I can identify and discuss signs and symptoms of breast, pelvic and testicular cancer. 
I can talk about the different medical support and help related to personal hygiene. 
I can outline safer sex practices e.g. communicate with partner, abstaining from any type of sexual activities, limit

I can provide examples of ethical behaviour in relationships.

I can outline the relationship between choices and resulting consequences. 
I can talk about my responsibility towards the environment. 
I can share my idea of a positive and healthy environment. 
I can define and discuss the idea and practice of sustainability. 
I can discuss current challenges related to sustainability issues.
