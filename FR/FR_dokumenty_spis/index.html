    Bioregion: Atlantic / Continental
    Statut de la réforme / Reform Status: Major reform 2025 — Programme national EVARS mandatory from September 2025

    System Overview¶

    France operates a highly centralised education system. Health education (Éducation à la santé) is not a standalone subject but is delivered as a cross-curricular theme across SVT (Life and Earth Sciences), EPS (Physical Education), and EMC (Moral and Civic Education). The structural framework is the Parcours éducatif de santé (PES), mandatory since 2016. Each school has a CESCE committee coordinating health and citizenship education. The major 2025 reform introduces the EVARS programme — a structured national programme for affective, relational and sexuality education (minimum 3 sessions per year per level), mandatory from the 2025–2026 school year.

    A. Législation primaire / Primary Legislation¶

    1. Code de l'éducation — Article L312-16 (Éducation à la sexualité)¶

    • Type: Primary legislation / Code de l'éducation
    • Year: 2001 (Loi du 4 juillet 2001, as amended)
    • Publisher: Légifrance / Assemblée nationale
    • Description: The foundational legal provision requiring at least 3 annual sessions of sexuality education at every level of schooling (primary, collège, lycée). Content covers biological, psycho-emotional, and legal/social dimensions. Updated in 2025 by the EVARS programme which builds on this obligation.
    • Access (Légifrance): https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006524759/

    2. Décret n° 2016-1855 du 26 décembre 2016 — Parcours éducatif de santé (PES)¶

    • Type: Decree / Décret
    • Year: 2016
    • Publisher: Ministère de l'Éducation nationale (MEN) / Légifrance
    • Description: Establishes the Parcours éducatif de santé (PES) as the organising framework for health education in all French schools, from maternelle to lycée. The PES has three axes: health education (psychosocial skills), prevention (addictions, harassment, mental health), and health protection (medical follow-up). This decree is the structural backbone of health education in France.
    • Access (Légifrance): https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000033728261/
    • Éduscol — PES page: https://eduscol.education.fr/1421/parcours-educatif-de-sante

    3. Décret n° 2022-540 du 12 avril 2022 — CESCE¶

    • Type: Decree / Décret
    • Year: 2022
    • Publisher: MEN / Légifrance
    • Description: Establishes the Comité d'éducation à la santé, à la citoyenneté et à l'environnement (CESCE) as a mandatory committee in every lycée and collège. Replaces the former CESC (adding environment dimension). The CESCE is composed of the head teacher, educational staff, students, parents, and external partners. It defines the school's health education action plan.
    • Access (Légifrance): https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000045609138/

    B. Programme national EVARS / National EVARS Programme¶

    4. Arrêté du 3 février 2025 — Programme national EVARS¶

    • Type: Ministerial order / Arrêté
    • Year: 2025 (published in JO 5 February 2025; mandatory from September 2025)
    • Publisher: Ministère de l'Éducation nationale et de la Jeunesse
    • Description: Establishes the Programme d'Éducation à la vie affective, relationnelle et à la sexualité (EVARS) — the most significant reform of French health education in decades. EVARS requires structured, progressive sessions at every school level. Content: the body; relationships and emotions; consent; STIs and contraception; digital media and pornography. Applies to all public schools and private schools under contract.
    • Access (Légifrance): https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000051075451
    • Éduscol — EVARS: https://eduscol.education.fr/4032/education-la-vie-affective-relationnelle-et-la-sexualite-evars

    C. Programmes des disciplines / Subject Curricula¶

    5. Programmes de SVT — Sciences de la Vie et de la Terre (Collège + Lycée)¶

    • Type: National curriculum / Programmes officiels (Bulletin Officiel)
    • Year: Various editions (most recent updates 2019–2023)
    • Publisher: MEN / Conseil Supérieur des Programmes (CSP) / Éduscol
    • Description: SVT is the primary subject carrier for health content: human biology, reproduction, immunity, nutrition, substance effects on the body. SVT programmes contain explicit health learning objectives at each level of collège (6e–3e) and lycée (Seconde–Terminale). Also addresses environmental health.
    • Access — all SVT programmes: https://eduscol.education.fr/programmesvt
    • Bulletin Officiel (BO) search: https://www.education.gouv.fr/bo/

    6. Programmes d'EMC — Enseignement Moral et Civique (Collège + Lycée, 2024 update)¶

    • Type: National curriculum / Programmes officiels (BO)
    • Year: 2024 (renewed programme, BO June 2024)
    • Publisher: MEN / CSP
    • Description: EMC addresses the values, rights, and social dimensions of health: consent, dignity, equality, citizenship. The 2024 update reinforces the connection between personal responsibility and collective health. EMC is closely linked with EVARS content on relationships and consent.
    • Access — EMC programmes: https://eduscol.education.fr/727/programmes-d-enseignement-moral-et-civique

    7. Programmes d'EPS — Éducation Physique et Sportive (Collège + Lycée)¶

    • Type: National curriculum / Programmes officiels (BO)
    • Year: 2015–2019 (Collège); 2019 (Lycée)
    • Publisher: MEN / CSP
    • Description: EPS is mandatory and contributes to the health education strand: physical activity, body awareness, wellbeing, emotional management through sport. The subject explicitly targets student health and lifestyle outcomes.
    • Access: https://eduscol.education.fr/programmeeps

    D. Données et rapports de recherche / Research Reports and Data¶

    8. Enquête EnCLASS 2022 — Enquête nationale en collèges et lycées chez les adolescents sur la santé et les substances¶

    • Nom complet / Full title: Enquête nationale en collèges et lycées chez les adolescents sur la santé et les substances (EnCLASS 2022)
    • Type: Research report / Rapport de recherche
    • Year: 2022–2024 (data 2022; published 2023–2024)
    • Publisher: Santé publique France / OFDT / EHESP
    • Description: France's national health behaviour survey for adolescents — the equivalent of HBSC for France. Covers tobacco, alcohol, cannabis, other substances, sexual health, mental health, and wellbeing among collège and lycée students. Key empirical reference for the EVARS reform.
    • Access (Santé publique France): https://www.santepubliquefrance.fr/determinants-de-sante/nutrition-et-activite-physique/articles/enclass

    9. Rapport IGESR 2021 — Éducation à la sexualité dans les écoles, collèges et lycées¶

    • Nom complet / Full title: La mise en œuvre de l'éducation à la sexualité dans les écoles, collèges et lycées
    • Type: Inspection report / Rapport d'inspection
    • Year: 2021
    • Publisher: Inspection générale de l'éducation, du sport et de la recherche (IGESR)
    • Description: Critical government inspection report documenting the chronic failure to implement the legally required 3-session annual sexuality education in French schools. Findings directly motivated the 2025 EVARS reform. Available through the MEN reports portal.
    • Access: https://www.education.gouv.fr/les-rapports-de-l-igsr-2021-323717

    E. Ressources pédagogiques nationales / National Teaching Resources¶

    10. Éduscol — Parcours éducatif de santé: ressources pour les équipes éducatives¶

    • Type: Teaching resources portal / Ressources pédagogiques
    • Year: 2016–present (continuously updated)
    • Publisher: Éduscol (MEN)
    • Description: The national pedagogical portal for the PES. Includes lesson plans, intervention models, links to approved external partners, and resources for each health theme (nutrition, mental health, addictions, sexual health). Also contains EVARS implementation guidance from 2025.
    • Access: https://eduscol.education.fr/1421/parcours-educatif-de-sante
