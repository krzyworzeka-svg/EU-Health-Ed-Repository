**Inclusion Criteria**

## Section 2: Thematic Analysis Deep Dive (OH/PH)

### 2.1 Preliminary Findings
A robust integration of "One Health" and "Planetary Health" concepts is visible in the Maltese policy documents, particularly through the lens of Sustainable Development, Home Economics, and PSCD (Personal, Social and Career Development):
* **Sustainable Dietary Choices:** Home Economics (HEcon) heavily integrates human dietary needs with ecological metrics (carbon footprint, food miles, organic agriculture).
* **Cross-Curricular Sustainable Development:** The core National Curriculum Framework (NCF 2012) mandates viewing human health and productivity as fundamentally dependent on a safer and just environment.
* **Traceability and Public Health:** Advanced syllabus topics emphasize food supply chain safety (HACCP) reflecting clear One Health intersections regarding food safety and disease vectors.
* **UN SDGs Integration:** The PSCD curriculum embeds Global Goals (Zero Hunger, Good Health and Well-being) teaching students to align personal well-being with global ecological and social sustainability.
