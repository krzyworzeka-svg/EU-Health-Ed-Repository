## Section 2: Thematic Analysis Deep Dive (OH/PH)

### 2.1 Preliminary Findings
A robust integration of "One Health" and "Planetary Health" concepts is visible in the Latvian policy documents, particularly through the lens of Sustainable Development (SD) and Environmental Health:
* **Pollution and Respiratory Health:** The Biology curriculum (LV_DOC_001) explicitly links air pollution (*gaisa piesārņojums*) to respiratory organ system impacts.
* **Environmental Citizenship and EcoHealth:** The Basic Education Standard (LV_DOC_002) mandates that pupils act responsibly in preserving environmental quality and sustainable use of natural resources alongside promoting their own health.
* **National Strategy on Anthropocene:** The UNECE report (LV_DOC_003) explicitly highlights "Education for Sustainable Development" methodologies for the "Anthropocene Age" and connects public health directly with environmental health (e.g., food, water quality, and pollution).
* **Systemic Health Framing:** Both the OECD report (LV_DOC_004) and national documents emphasize the integration of sustainable development and public health as core competencies for social development.
