## Section 2: Thematic Analysis Deep Dive (OH/PH)

### 2.1 Preliminary Findings
A robust integration of "One Health" and "Planetary Health" concepts is visible in the Lithuanian policy documents, particularly through the lens of Sustainable Development and Public Health:
* **Public Health and Environment:** The General Curriculum (LT_DOC_002) explicitly mandates analyzing the impact of lifestyle and environment on personal and public health.
* **Sustainable Development as a Statutory Goal:** The Law on Education (LT_DOC_001) frames environmental sustainability as a co-requisite with human and economic development.
* **Institutionalizing Public Health:** The Law on Education integrates municipal public health care directly into the school system, bridging traditional education with formal health interventions.
* **EcoHealth Frameworks:** The General Education Plans (LT_DOC_003) allow schools to adopt a distinctive "Ecology and Environmental Technologies" pedagogical system, acting as an incubator for advanced Planetary Health education.
