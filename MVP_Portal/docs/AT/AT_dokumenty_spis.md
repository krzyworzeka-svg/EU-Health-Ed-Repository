## System Overview

Austria has a **partially centralised** education system: the federal level defines the *Lehrplan* (national curriculum), while schools and teachers have implementation autonomy. Health education (*Gesundheitsbildung*) is delivered as a **cross-curricular theme** (*Unterrichtsprinzip Gesundheitserziehung*) and through specific subjects, primarily **Biologie und Umweltkunde (BuS)** and **Sexualpädagogik**. The **Lehrplan 2023** is a significant reform introducing competence-based learning across all subjects including health content. The national health education agency is **GIVE (Gesundheit, Information, Vernetzung, Entwicklung)** in cooperation with the Ministry of Education (BMBWF).

## A. Bundesebene — Rechtsrahmen / Federal Legal Framework

### 1. Bundesgesetz über die Ordnung von Unterricht und Erziehung — *Schulunterrichtsgesetz (SchUG)*

- **Type:** Federal law / Bundesgesetz | **Year:** 1974 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Bundesministerium für Bildung, Wissenschaft und Forschung (BMBWF)
- **Description:** The foundational law regulating schooling in Austria, providing the legal basis for the national curriculum (*Lehrplan*) and establishing the cross-curricular *Unterrichtsprinzipien* (teaching principles), of which *Gesundheitserziehung* (health education) and *Sexualerziehung* (sex education) are official ones.
- **Access (RIS):** [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10009276](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10009276)

---

### 2. Lehrplan 2023 — *Neuer Lehrplan für die Mittelschule / AHS*

- **Type:** National curriculum / Bundesgesetzlich verordneter Lehrplan | **Year:** 2023 (phased rollout 2023–2025)
- **Publisher:** BMBWF
- **Description:** Austria's major curriculum reform, implementing competence-based learning across all subjects. Includes updated health and biology content for secondary schools (*Mittelschule* and *AHS*). The Lehrplan 2023 is key for understanding how health topics (nutrition, sexuality, mental health, environmental health) are addressed in the current Austrian school system.
- **Access (BMBWF):** [https://www.bmbwf.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulpraxis/lp.html](https://www.bmbwf.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulpraxis/lp.html)
- **Lehrpläne online:** [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at)

---

### 3. Unterrichtsprinzip Gesundheitserziehung — *Rundschreiben BMBWF*

- **Type:** Ministerial circular / Unterrichtsprinzip | **Year:** Various (most recent: current Lehrplan 2023)
- **Publisher:** BMBWF
- **Description:** Health education in Austria is an **official cross-curricular teaching principle** (*Unterrichtsprinzip*), meaning all teachers at all levels are expected to integrate health topics into their teaching. The circular defines the scope: physical health, mental health, social health, nutrition, substance prevention, environmental health.
- **Access (BMBWF — Unterrichtsprinzipien):** [https://www.bmbwf.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulpraxis/unterrichtsprinzipien.html](https://www.bmbwf.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulpraxis/unterrichtsprinzipien.html)

## B. Curriculum — Hauptfächer / Main Subject Curricula

### 4. Lehrplan Biologie und Umweltkunde (BuS) — Sekundarstufe I und II

- **Type:** Subject curriculum | **Year:** 2023 (within Lehrplan 2023)
- **Publisher:** BMBWF
- **Description:** *Biologie und Umweltkunde* is the primary subject carrier for health content in Austrian secondary schools: human biology, reproduction, sexual health, nutrition, environmental health, ecology. The 2023 update emphasises competence-based learning and One Health / planetary health connections.
- **Access:** Embedded in Lehrplan 2023 (see above)

---

### 5. Sexualpädagogik — *Rahmenrichtlinien und Materialien* (GIVE, current)

- **Type:** Teaching guidelines / Rahmenrichtlinien | **Year:** Current
- **Publisher:** GIVE (Gesundheit, Information, Vernetzung, Entwicklung) / BMBWF
- **Description:** GIVE provides the national reference framework for *Sexualpädagogik* (sexuality pedagogy) in Austrian schools. Includes age-appropriate content standards, approved methodologies, and guidance on sensitive topics. GIVE also certifies external organizations allowed to deliver sexuality education sessions in schools.
- **Access (GIVE):** [https://www.give.or.at](https://www.give.or.at)
