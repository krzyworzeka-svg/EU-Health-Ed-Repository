## System Overview

Slovakia uses a **two-level curriculum system** similar to the Czech Republic: the **ŠVP** (*Štátny vzdelávací program* — State Educational Programme) defines national objectives, and schools develop their own **ŠkVP** (*Školský vzdelávací program*). Health education is not a standalone subject at most levels — it is integrated into **Biology**, **Physical Education**, and **Ethics/Religious Education** (*Etická výchova / Náboženská výchova*). A distinctive feature is the strong role of the Catholic Church in Slovak education, which influences the content and methodology of sexuality education. The **ŠPÚ** (State Pedagogical Institute) is the key curriculum authority.

## A. Právny rámec / Legal Framework

### 1. Zákon č. 245/2008 Z.z. — *Zákon o výchove a vzdelávaní* (Education Act)

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Zákon | **Year:** 2008 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Národná rada Slovenskej republiky / MŠVVaŠ SR
- **Description:** The foundational Slovak education law. Establishes the ŠVP/ŠkVP two-level system. Provides legal basis for health education content requirements. Includes provisions regarding parental rights regarding sensitive content (opt-out for ethics/religious education and related health content).
- **Access (slov-lex.sk):** [https://www.slov-lex.sk/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2008/245/](https://www.slov-lex.sk/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2008/245/)

---

### 2. Štátny vzdelávací program (ŠVP) — *Základná škola + Gymnázium* (ŠPÚ)

- **Plný názov / Full title:** Štátny vzdelávací program pre základné školy / gymnáziá
- **Type:** State Educational Programme | **Year:** 2015 (base); updates 2021–2023
- **Publisher:** Štátny pedagogický ústav (ŠPÚ) / MŠVVaŠ SR
- **Description:** The national framework defining curriculum for Slovak basic schools and gymnasia. Health content is embedded across: Biology (primary carrier), Physical Education, and Ethics/Religious Education. The ŠVP includes a cross-curricular theme *Ochrana života a zdravia* (Protection of Life and Health).
- **Access (ŠPÚ):** [https://www.statpedu.sk/sk/svp/](https://www.statpedu.sk/sk/svp/)
