# Sweden – Health Education Curriculum Content (Lgr22 + Gy25)

## 1. Overview: Transversal Health Education Model

Sweden does **not have a standalone "Health Education" subject**. Instead, health content is distributed across several mandatory subjects, with two primary carriers:

1. **Idrott och hälsa (PE and Health)** – mandatory for all students, grades 1–12
2. **Sexualitet, samtycke och relationer (Sexuality, Consent and Relationships)** – mandatory cross-cutting theme, distributed across multiple subjects

Secondary carriers:
- **Biologi** (Biology) – human biology, diseases, ecology
- **Hem- och konsumentkunskap** (Home and Consumer Studies) – nutrition
- **Samhällskunskap** (Civics/Social Studies) – public health, health system
- **Religionskunskap** (Religious Studies) – ethics, values, body

---

## 2. Idrott och hälsa (PE and Health) – Primary Health Carrier

### 2.1 Subject Overview

**Idrott och hälsa** is mandatory for ALL Swedish students from grade 1 through the end of gymnasieskola. Its name explicitly includes "health" (hälsa), and health literacy is a core aim alongside physical fitness.

### 2.2 Grundskola Content (Lgr22, grades 7–9)

**Strand 1: Physical Activity and Movement**
- Range of movement activities: individual sports, team sports, outdoor/friluftsliv
- Training methods: endurance, strength, flexibility, coordination
- Nordic outdoor tradition (*friluftsliv*): nature-based activity as health resource

**Strand 2: Health, Lifestyle and Well-being (Hälsa, levnadsvanor och välbefinnande)**
- Factors affecting health: physical activity, diet, sleep, stress, social relationships
- Physical and mental health connections: how regular exercise affects mood, anxiety, cognition
- Body image and ideals: critical analysis of body norms; risk of eating disorders and doping
- Ergonomics: posture, lifting, workplace health principles

**Strand 3: Safety and Emergency Response**
- Risk assessment in physical activities
- Basic first aid: HLR (CPR); calling 112; common injuries

### 2.3 Gymnasieskola Content (Gy25, from July 2025)

The new Gy25 restructures Idrott och hälsa from a course-based to a subject-based grading system with three main areas:

**Area 1: Kroppslig förmåga (Physical Capacity)**
- Anatomy and physiology: muscles, cardiovascular system, respiratory system
- Training theory: periodisation, overtraining, recovery
- Use of digital health tools: pulse monitors, training apps, health tracking

**Area 2: Levnadsvanor och hälsa (Lifestyle and Health)**
- Diet and nutrition for health and performance
- Sleep quality: adolescent sleep needs; consequences of deprivation
- Stress management strategies: physiological and psychological approaches
- Body ideals and norms: media influence; eating disorders; doping (anabola steroider)
- Mental health: anxiety, depression – connections to physical activity patterns

**Area 3: Riskhantering och ergonomi (Risk Management and Ergonomics)**
- Workplace ergonomics: relevant for both school and future work
- First aid including HLR (CPR)
- Safety protocols in sport and physical activity

## 3. Sexualitet, samtycke och relationer (Cross-Cutting Theme)

### 3.1 legal and Historical Basis

**Sweden was the FIRST country in the world to introduce mandatory sex education (1955)**. In Lgr22 (2022), the topic was renamed from *"sex- och samlevnadsundervisning"* to **"sexualitet, samtycke och relationer"** – placing CONSENT at the centre.

**Key principle:** This is not a single subject or class – it must be addressed **repeatedly and continuously** throughout schooling, integrated across multiple subjects.

### 3.2 Subjects Responsible (Multi-Disciplinary)

| Subject | Health/Sexuality Topics Covered |
|---|---|
| **Biologi** | Anatomy; reproduction; puberty; contraception; STIs; HPV vaccination |
| **Samhällskunskap** | LGBTQI+ rights; gender equality; public health; reproductive rights |
| **Religionskunskap** | Ethics of relationships; cultural/religious perspectives on sexuality |
| **Historia** | History of LGBTQI+ rights; history of women's reproductive rights |
| **Idrott och hälsa** | Body image; physical aspects of health; eating disorders |
| **Svenska** (Swedish language) | Critical reading of texts about gender, sexuality, relationships |

### 3.3 Content Topics (Grundskola, Grades 7–9)

**Sexual Development and Health:**
- Puberty: physical and emotional changes; individual variation
- Human anatomy for reproduction; menstrual cycle
- Contraception choices: condom (also for STI prevention), hormonal methods, emergency contraception
- STIs: chlamydia (most common in Sweden), HIV, gonorrhea, HPV
- HPV vaccination: part of Swedish national vaccination program for all girls AND boys (since 2020)

**Consent (Samtycke) – Central Theme:**
- What consent means: explicit, informed, voluntary, revocable
- Swedish law (Brottsbalken, amended 2018): "samtyckeslag" – criminal law requires explicit consent for sexual activity
- Online consent: sexting; sharing images without permission (illegal in Sweden)
- Recognizing coercion and pressure in relationships

**Relationships and Norms:**
- Healthy vs. unhealthy relationships: communication, boundaries, red flags
- LGBTQI+ identities: sexual orientation spectrum; gender identity; gender expression
- Norm critique (*normkritik*): questioning heteronormativity; stereotypes
- Honor-based violence (*hedersrelaterat våld*): definitions; rights to seek help (important in Swedish context)

**Digital and Media Literacy:**
- Critical analysis of how relationships and sexuality are portrayed in media and pornography
- Pornography ≠ real sex: unrealistic expectations; coercion in pornography industry
- Social media and body image: filters; comparison culture; self-worth

### 3.4 Content Topics (Gymnasieskola, Ages 16–19)

**Advanced Sexual Health:**
- Contraception for adults: long-acting reversible contraception (LARC); IUD; combined methods
- STI epidemiology in Sweden (Folkhälsomyndigheten data): chlamydia trends; gonorrhea increases
- Access to free STI testing in youth clinics (*ungdomsmottagning*) – no parental consent needed
- Abortion rights: legal in Sweden up to 18 weeks; free on demand; no parental consent required for minors

**Rights and Society:**
- Swedish abortion law: free choice (fri abort) since 1974 – 50+ year anniversary
- LGBTQI+ legal rights in Sweden: same-sex marriage (2009); joint adoption; legal gender change
- Reproductive technology: IVF; surrogacy (not legal commercially in Sweden)

**Violence Prevention and Support:**
- Intimate partner violence: cycle of abuse; legal resources (Polisen, socialtjänsten)
- Sexual harassment in working life: Diskrimineringslagen
- Resources: RFSL; BRIS (children's rights organisation); 1177 (health line)

## 4. Biologi – Secondary Health Carrier

### 4.1 Grundskola Biology (Lgr22, Grades 7–9)

**Human Body and Health:**
- Organ systems: digestive, respiratory, circulatory, nervous, endocrine, reproductive
- Disease mechanisms: bacteria vs. viruses vs. parasites; immune response
- Vaccines and vaccination policy (Sweden's national vaccination programme)
- Non-communicable diseases: cardiovascular disease risk factors; type 2 diabetes; cancer prevention
- Substance use: effects of alcohol, tobacco, cannabis on the body and brain

**Ecology and Environment (One Health connection):**
- Ecosystems; food webs; biodiversity
- Environmental pollution and human health
- Climate change: effects on ecosystems; introduction of new vector-borne diseases (fästingar/ticks relevant in Sweden)
- Antibiotic resistance (AMR): Swedish *Folkhälsomyndigheten* has national strategy

### 4.2 Gymnasieskola Biology

- Genetics and hereditary diseases
- Immunology: innate vs. adaptive immunity; autoimmune diseases
- Biotechnology: CRISPR; GMOs; bioethics
- Environmental biology: ecosystem services; planetary boundaries

## 5. Hem- och konsumentkunskap (Health via Food and Home)

This mandatory grundskola subject covers:
- Nutrition knowledge: macronutrients, vitamins, minerals; reading food labels
- **Sustainable food**: environmental impact of food choices; plant-based diet; food waste
- Food allergies and intolerances
- Budget and consumer rights

**Planetary Health connection**: Swedish schools teach sustainable food choices as part of this compulsory subject – one of the strongest practical Planetary Health connections in the curriculum.
