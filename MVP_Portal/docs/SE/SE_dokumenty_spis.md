## System Overview

Sweden has a **centralised but school-autonomous** education system managed by *Skolverket* (Swedish National Agency for Education). Health education is integrated across subjects, primarily through **Idrott och hälsa** (Physical Education and Health — mandatory at all levels) and through the **cross-curricular theme "Sexualitet, samtycke och relationer"** (Sexuality, consent and relationships), which is explicitly addressed in all relevant subjects since the **Lgr22** curriculum reform. The **Elevhälsa** (student health team) model — including school counsellors, nurses, psychologists, and social workers — is a strong institutional feature. Sweden is a global reference point for progressive, rights-based sexuality and health education.

## A. Lag och förordning / Legal Framework

### 1. Skollagen (2010:800) — *The Education Act*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Lag | **Year:** 2010 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Riksdagen / Utbildningsdepartementet
- **Description:** The foundational education law. Establishes the requirements for *Elevhälsa* (student health service) including school nurses, counsellors, and psychologists as mandatory in all schools. Defines the purposes of education including health, wellbeing, democratic values, and equality. Provides the legal basis for Skolverket's curriculum authority.
- **Access (riksdagen.se):** [https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/skollag-2010800_sfs-2010-800/](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/skollag-2010800_sfs-2010-800/)

---

### 2. Diskrimineringslagen (2008:567) — relevance for health education

- **Type:** Anti-discrimination law | **Year:** 2008
- **Publisher:** Riksdagen
- **Description:** Sweden's Discrimination Act prohibits discrimination on grounds including gender, gender identity/expression, and sexual orientation. Schools are legally required to actively promote equality and prevent discrimination — a key legal underpinning for inclusive LGBTQ+ health and sexuality education.
- **Access:** [https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)

## B. Läroplaner och ämnesplaner / National Curricula

### 3. Lgr22 — *Läroplan för grundskolan 2022*

- **Fullständigt namn / Full title:** Läroplan för grundskolan, förskoleklassen och fritidshemmet 2022 (Lgr22)
- **Type:** National curriculum | **Year:** 2022 (in force from 1 July 2022)
- **Publisher:** Skolverket
- **Description:** The current national curriculum for Swedish compulsory school (grades 1–9). Key health education change: introduces **"Sexualitet, samtycke och relationer"** as an explicit cross-curricular perspective integrated into relevant subjects (Biology, Social Studies, Physical Education and Health, etc.) at all grade levels. Also establishes health competency in *Idrott och hälsa* as a core strand.
- **Access (Skolverket):** [https://www.skolverket.se/publikationsserier/styrdokument/2022/laroplan-for-grundskolan-forskoleklassen-och-fritidshemmet-lgr22](https://www.skolverket.se/publikationsserier/styrdokument/2022/laroplan-for-grundskolan-forskoleklassen-och-fritidshemmet-lgr22)
- **Direct PDF:** [https://www.skolverket.se/download/18.4fc05a3f164131a74181056/1620730985819/pdf4541.pdf](https://www.skolverket.se/download/18.4fc05a3f164131a74181056/1620730985819/pdf4541.pdf)

---

### 4. Gy25 — *Läroplan för gymnasieskolan 2025*

- **Fullständigt namn / Full title:** Läroplan för gymnasieskolan 2025 (Gy25)
- **Type:** National curriculum | **Year:** 2025 (in force from July 2025)
- **Publisher:** Skolverket
- **Description:** The new national curriculum for Swedish upper secondary school (*gymnasieskolan*). Continues and deepens the Lgr22 approach to health and sexuality education. Health content at upper secondary level is primarily through *Idrott och hälsa* (mandatory) and Natural Science subjects. "Sexualitet, samtycke och relationer" perspective is maintained.
- **Access (Skolverket):** [https://www.skolverket.se/regler-och-ansvar/lagar-och-regler/laroplanerna-for-skolan](https://www.skolverket.se/regler-och-ansvar/lagar-och-regler/laroplanerna-for-skolan)

## C. Hälsodata / Health Data

### 5. Skolans hälsa — *Nationell undersökning om elevers hälsa och välbefinnande* (Skolverket/Folkhälsomyndigheten)

- **Type:** National health survey | **Year:** Regular monitoring
- **Publisher:** Folkhälsomyndigheten (Public Health Agency of Sweden)
- **Description:** Sweden's national monitoring of student health and wellbeing. Data on physical health, mental health, sexual health, and substance use among Swedish school-aged children. Key reference for curriculum development and Elevhälsa policy.
- **Access (Folkhälsomyndigheten):** [https://www.folkhalsomyndigheten.se/folkhalsorapportering-statistik/statistikdatabaser-och-visualisering/skolbarns-halsa/](https://www.folkhalsomyndigheten.se/folkhalsorapportering-statistik/statistikdatabaser-och-visualisering/skolbarns-halsa/)
