## System Overview

Latvia has undergone a significant education reform through the **Skola2030** programme, fully implemented in 2023. The reform introduced a **competence-based curriculum** (*kompetenču pieeja*) across all subjects. Health education (*Veselības mācība*) is integrated into multiple subject areas, particularly **Biology**, **Physical Education** (*Sports un veselība*), and **Social Sciences** (*Sociālās zinātnes*). Latvia has no standalone health education subject at secondary level, but the Skola2030 reform significantly strengthened health literacy as a competency. The **Slimību profilakses un kontroles centrs (SPKC)** provides health monitoring data.

### 2. Skola2030 — *Mācību saturs un pieejas reforma*

- **Pilnais nosaukums / Full title:** Skola2030 — Kompetenču pieeja mācību saturā
- **Type:** National curriculum reform | **Year:** 2017–2023 (phased implementation, full by 2023)
- **Publisher:** Izglītības un zinātnes ministrija (IZM) / VISC (National Centre for Education)
- **Description:** Latvia's national curriculum reform introducing competence-based learning across all subjects. Health literacy is integrated as a key competency. The reform defines learning outcomes for Biology and Physical Education that explicitly include health content: body and health, reproduction, substance prevention, environmental health, wellbeing. Comprehensive documentation available at Skola2030 portal.
- **Access (Skola2030):** [https://www.skola2030.lv](https://www.skola2030.lv)
- **Curriculum materials (VISC):** [https://www.visc.gov.lv/lv/macibu-saturs](https://www.visc.gov.lv/lv/macibu-saturs)
