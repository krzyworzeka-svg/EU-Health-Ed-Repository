## System Overview

Greece has a **highly centralised** education system managed by the **Υπουργείο Παιδείας και Θρησκευμάτων** (Ministry of Education and Religious Affairs). Health education (*Αγωγή Υγείας*) is not a standalone subject but is delivered as a **cross-curricular theme** integrated into Biology (*Βιολογία*), Physical Education (*Φυσική Αγωγή*), and other subjects. A significant innovation was the introduction of **Skills Labs** (*Εργαστήρια Δεξιοτήτων*) in 2021–2022 — mandatory weekly sessions focused on life skills, emotional intelligence, digital literacy, and health. The Greek Orthodox Church has historically exerted significant influence on sensitive health content (particularly sexuality education). The **ΕΟΔΥ** (National Public Health Organisation) provides health data.

## A. Νομικό πλαίσιο / Legal Framework

### 1. Νόμος 4823/2021 — *Νέο Σχολείο: Δεξιότητες Ζωής (Skills Labs)*

- **Πλήρης τίτλος / Full title:** Νόμος 4823/2021 — Αναβάθμιση του σχολείου, ενδυνάμωση των εκπαιδευτικών και άλλες διατάξεις
- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Νόμος | **Year:** 2021
- **Publisher:** Ελληνική Βουλή / ΦΕΚ (Government Gazette)
- **Description:** Introduces the **Skills Labs** (*Εργαστήρια Δεξιοτήτων*) as mandatory sessions in Greek secondary schools. Skills Labs include life skills, emotional wellbeing, digital literacy, environmental awareness, and health topics. This is the most significant recent reform affecting health education in Greece.
- **Access (ΦΕΚ):** [https://www.et.gr](https://www.et.gr)
- **Ministry of Education:** [https://www.minedu.gov.gr](https://www.minedu.gov.gr)
