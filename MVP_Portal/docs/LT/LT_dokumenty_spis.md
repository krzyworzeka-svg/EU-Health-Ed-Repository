## System Overview

Lithuania has a **centralised curriculum** managed by the **Švietimo, mokslo ir sporto ministerija** (Ministry of Education, Science and Sports). Health education is delivered through the **GIUP** (*Gyvenimo įgūdžių ugdymo programa* — Life Skills Education Programme) which is integrated across several subjects, particularly **Biologija** (Biology) and **Kūno kultūra** (Physical Education). Lithuania has made significant investments in life skills and mental health education in recent years, partly driven by high suicide rates. The **Higienos institutas** and **NVSC** (National Public Health Centre) provide health data.

## A. Teisinis pagrindas / Legal Framework

### 1. Lietuvos Respublikos Švietimo įstatymas — *Education Law*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Įstatymas | **Year:** 1991 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Lietuvos Respublikos Seimas
- **Description:** The foundational Lithuanian education law. Provides the legal basis for curriculum frameworks, mandatory subjects, and health education integration. Defines the structure of the Lithuanian school system.
- **Access (e-tar.lt):** [https://www.e-tar.lt/portal/legalAct.html?documentId=09cfdb00d8fb11e397b5c02d3197f382](https://www.e-tar.lt/portal/legalAct.html?documentId=09cfdb00d8fb11e397b5c02d3197f382)

### 2. Bendrojo ugdymo programos (BUP) 2022 — *General Education Programmes*

- **Type:** National curriculum / Bendrojo ugdymo programos | **Year:** 2022
- **Publisher:** Švietimo, mokslo ir sporto ministerija / Ugdymo plėtotės centras (UPC)
- **Description:** The 2022 General Education Programmes define curriculum objectives across all subjects. Health-related content is addressed in Biology, Physical Education, and through the GIUP cross-curricular programme. The 2022 reform strengthened mental health and life skills competencies.
- **Access (UPC):** [https://www.upc.smm.lt/ugdymas/bendrojo-ugdymo-programos/](https://www.upc.smm.lt/ugdymas/bendrojo-ugdymo-programos/)

## B. Sveikatos duomenys / Health Data

### 3. HBSC Lietuva 2022 — Health Behaviour in School-aged Children

- **Type:** Research report | **Year:** 2022 | **Publisher:** Higienos institutas / WHO
- **Description:** Lithuania's HBSC study contribution. Data on health behaviours, wellbeing, and risk behaviours of Lithuanian adolescents. Notable for data on mental health — Lithuania has historically high rates of youth suicide, driving significant policy attention to mental health education.
- **Access (Higienos institutas):** [https://www.hi.lt/en/hbsc.html](https://www.hi.lt/en/hbsc.html)
