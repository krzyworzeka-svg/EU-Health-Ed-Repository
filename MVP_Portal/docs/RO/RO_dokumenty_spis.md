## System Overview

Romania has a **centralised education system** managed by the **Ministerul Educației**. **Educație pentru sănătate** (Health Education) is a mandatory standalone subject in Romanian schools — one of the few countries in Central-Eastern Europe with a dedicated health education subject. However, implementation quality varies significantly. The new **Legea educației naționale nr. 198/2023** introduced significant reforms to the curriculum framework. Health education is also delivered through **Biologie** and **Consiliere și orientare** (Guidance and Counselling). Romania has been a site of significant public debate about sex education content, with campaigns both for and against comprehensive sexuality education. The **INSP** (National Institute of Public Health) is the key health data organisation.

## A. Cadru legal / Legal Framework

### 1. Legea educației naționale nr. 198/2023

- **Titlul complet / Full title:** Legea educației naționale nr. 198/2023
- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Lege | **Year:** 2023
- **Publisher:** Parlamentul României / Ministerul Educației
- **Description:** Romania's new Education Law replacing the previous 2011 law. Maintains health education as a mandatory subject and defines the new framework for curriculum development. Introduces competence-based learning and updated provisions for health and wellbeing in schools.
- **Access (legislatie.just.ro):** [https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/273805](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/273805)
- **Monitorul Oficial:** [https://www.monitoruloficial.ro/](https://www.monitoruloficial.ro/)

### 2. Curriculum național — *Educație pentru sănătate* (Ministerul Educației)

- **Type:** National curriculum | **Year:** Current (updated 2023–2024)
- **Publisher:** Ministerul Educației / CNPEE (National Centre for Evaluation and Examination)
- **Description:** The official curriculum for the mandatory *Educație pentru sănătate* subject. Covers: physical health, mental health, sexual and reproductive health, nutrition, substance prevention, first aid, environmental health. The curriculum has been the subject of ongoing revision in the context of public debates about sex education content.
- **Access (edu.ro):** [https://www.edu.ro/curricul%C4%83m-na%C5%A3ional](https://www.edu.ro/curricul%C4%83m-na%C5%A3ional)
