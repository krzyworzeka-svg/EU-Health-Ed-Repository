# Denmark – Sundhedsundervisning: Curriculum Content (Folkeskole + Gymnasium)

## 1. Overview: Transversal Health Education Model

Like Sweden, Denmark has **no standalone "Health Education" subject**. instead, health content is distributed across:

| Subject/Topic | Type | Health content |
|---|---|---|
| **Sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab** | Mandatory topic (emne) | Sexual health, body, family, well-being |
| **Idræt** (Physical Education) | Mandatory subject | Physical health, fitness, lifestyle |
| **Biologi** (Biology) | Mandatory subject (7–9 kl.) | Human biology, disease, ecology |
| **Samfundsfag** (Social Studies) | Mandatory subject | Public health, healthcare system |
| **Natur/teknologi** (Nature/Technology) | Lower school (1–6 kl.) | Body awareness, basic health |

## 2. Sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab (HEALTH + SEX ED TOPIC)

### 2.1 Topic Overview

**Full Danish name:** Sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab  
**English:** Health and Sexual Education and Family Studies  
**Type:** Mandatory *emne* (topic), not a subject with its own hours  
**Implementation:** Distributed across multiple subjects; school principal decides how

### 2.2 Competency Domains

The topic has **two core competency domains (kompetenceområder)**:

**Kompetenceområde 1: Sundhed og trivsel (Health and Well-being)**
- Understanding health as a multidimensional concept (physical, mental, social)
- Individual and collective dimension of health
- Health promotion and prevention

**Kompetenceområde 2: Køn, krop og seksualitet (Gender, Body and Sexuality)**
- Bodily development; puberty; anatomy
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Relationships and intimacy

### 2.3 Content by Age Group (Focus: 7th–9th Grade)

**Sundhed og trivsel (Health and Well-being):**

*Knowledge and Understanding:*
- Health determinants: genetics, lifestyle, social environment, access to healthcare
- Physical health: diet, sleep, physical activity, body weight
- Mental health: stress management; emotions; self-esteem; resilience
- Substance use: alcohol, tobacco, cannabis – effects and prevention
- Disease and immunity: bacteria, viruses, vaccines; how the immune system works
- The Danish healthcare system (*sundhedsvæsnet*): GP, hospital, emergency care

*Skills (færdigheder):*
- Critically evaluate health information (media, internet)
- Set personal health goals
- Seek help appropriately (GP, sundhedsplejerske, emergency services)

*Competency (kompetencer):*
- Make informed, autonomous health choices
- Support others' health and well-being
- Participate in health-promoting communities

**Køn, krop og seksualitet (Gender, Body and Sexuality):**

*Knowledge and Understanding:*
- Puberty: physical and emotional changes; individual variation; early vs. late development
- Anatomy: male and female reproductive systems; menstrual cycle
- Sexual health: contraception methods (condom, pill, IUD, emergency); STIs (chlamydia, HIV, HPV, herpes)
- HPV vaccination (national programme; offered to all 12-year-old girls and boys since 2020)
- Consent (*samtykke*): explicit, voluntary, revocable; Danish law perspective
- Sexual orientation and gender identity: spectrum approach; diversity as norm
- Online safety: sexting; image sharing; digital consent; exploitation
- **Pornografi og medie-kritik** (Pornography and media criticism): pornography vs. real relationships; unrealistic expectations
- Healthy vs. unhealthy relationships: communication, boundaries, red flags, coercion

*Skills:*
- Communicate about sexual health, boundaries and consent
- Navigate LGBTQ+ questions with respect and knowledge
- Access sexual health services (læge, jordemoder, familieplanlegging)
- Report unwanted sexual contact (Politi, Børns Vilkår helpline)

*Competency:*
- Develop a personal sense of sexual identity
- Develop respectful relationships based on consent
- Support peers in sexual health matters

### 2.4 Family Studies Component

- Family structures in contemporary Denmark (diverse: single-parent, same-sex, blended)
- Children's rights in families (Danish Children's Act *Barnets lov* 2023)
- Reproductive choices: pregnancy, birth, adoption, assisted reproduction (IVF)
- Family economics and budget basics

## 3. Gymnasium Seksualundervisning (Mandatory from 2023/24)

### 3.1 Reform Context

Prior to 2023, gymnasium students (16–19 years) had **no mandatory sex education**. The 2021 government investment of 15 million DKK and advocacy by students and *Sex og Samfund* led to legislation requiring mandatory seksualundervisning in all gymnasium programmes from **school year 2023/24**.

### 3.2 Content Topics (Gymnasium Level)

**Samtykke og grænser (Consent and Boundaries):**
- Danish legal definition of consent
- Situations where consent is ambiguous; power dynamics
- Digital contexts: image sharing; social media boundaries
- Reporting sexual assault: rights, resources, police process

**Seksuel sundhed (Sexual Health):**
- Contraception for adults: long-acting options (IUD, hormonal implant); combined methods
- STI epidemiology in Denmark: chlamydia rates; gonorrhea resistance; syphilis resurgence
- Free STI testing: available through GP; specific clinics in larger cities
- Abortion: free on demand in Denmark up to week 12 (since 1973); expanded to week 22 in 2023 (*Abortloven reform*)

**Seksuel identitet og LGBTQ+ (Sexual Identity and LGBTQ+):**
- Gender identity and gender dysphoria
- Legal gender change in Denmark (possible without surgery since 2014 – pioneering legislation)
- Coming out: support resources; LGBTQ+ organisations (LGBT+ Danmark)

## 4. Idræt (Physical Education and Health)

### 4.1 Folkeskole Idræt (Grades 1–9)

**Mandatory for all students; integrates health perspective:**
- Movement and fitness: varied sports; endurance, strength, coordination
- Health knowledge: how physical activity benefits body and mind
- Body awareness: anatomy of movement; injury prevention
- **Friluftsliv**: outdoor activities (like Swedish model); nature as health resource
- Social health: team sports; fair play; inclusion

**Health topics covered:**
- Effects of lifestyle on health
- Body image and eating disorders (brief coverage)
- First aid basics: emergency response; CPR awareness

### 4.2 Gymnasium Idræt (Mandatory in most programmes)

**More advanced health perspective:**
- Exercise physiology: cardiovascular training; VO2 max; recovery
- Health behaviour change theory: why people do or don't exercise
- Mental health and physical activity: research evidence
- Nutrition science: energy balance; macronutrients; sports nutrition
- Ergonomics: work health; sedentary behaviour
- **Sundhedspolitik** (health policy): Danish public health strategy; welfare state model

## 5. Biologi (Biology) – Secondary Health Carrier

### 5.1 Folkeskole Biology (Grades 7–9)

**Human body and health:**
- Organ systems: most major systems covered
- Disease: infectious disease mechanisms; bacteria, viruses, immune response
- Vaccination: Danish national vaccination programme
- Chronic diseases: diabetes, cardiovascular disease – lifestyle factors
- Substance use: effects on the body (alcohol, tobacco, cannabis)

**Ecology and environment (One Health relevant):**
- Ecosystems; food webs; biodiversity
- Environmental pollution and human health
- Antibiotic resistance (AMR): use and overuse; resistance mechanisms
- Climate change: impacts on ecosystems (very briefly)
- **Zoonotic diseases**: background coverage (parasites, bacteria from animals)

### 5.2 STX/HF Biology (Gymnasium)

Available at multiple levels (Basic, Advanced):
- Genetics and heredity
- Immunology and disease
- Biotechnology: CRISPR, GMOs, bioethics
- Ecology: ecosystem services; climate interactions
- **Evolution**: antibiotic resistance as evolution in action (AMR)
