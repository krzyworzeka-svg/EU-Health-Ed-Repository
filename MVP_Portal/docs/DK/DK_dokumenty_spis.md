## System Overview

Denmark has a **centralised but school-autonomous** education system. The subject **Sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab (SSF)** — Health, Sex and Family Education — is a **mandatory subject** in the *Folkeskole* (primary/lower secondary, grades 1–9). At upper secondary (*Gymnasium*), health content is integrated across subjects, particularly Biology and Social Studies. The **Undervisningsministeriet** (Ministry of Children and Education) sets national curriculum objectives, but schools have considerable pedagogical autonomy. Denmark is notable for its **strong school health service** (*Sundhedsplejerske* — school health nurse present in all schools) and evidence-based approach to health education.

## A. Lovgrundlag / Legal Framework

### 1. Folkeskoleloven — *Lov om folkeskolen* (LBK nr. 1510 af 14/12/2023)

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Lov | **Year:** 1993 (latest consolidated 2023)
- **Publisher:** Undervisningsministeriet / Retsinformation
- **Description:** The foundational law governing the Danish *Folkeskole* (municipal primary and lower secondary school). Establishes SSF as a mandatory subject for all students. Defines the overall purpose of the *Folkeskole* including health, wellbeing, and personal development as central goals.
- **Access (Retsinformation):** [https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/1510](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/1510)

---

### 2. Bekendtgørelse om Folkeskolens undervisning i sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab

- **Type:** Ministerial order / Bekendtgørelse | **Year:** Current
- **Publisher:** Undervisningsministeriet
- **Description:** The specific ministerial order defining the objectives, content, and time allocation for SSF in the *Folkeskole*. Covers grades 1–9, with increasing depth and complexity. Content areas include: body and development, relationships and sexuality, family structures, mental health, and lifestyle choices.
- **Access (EMU — national education portal):** [https://www.emu.dk/modul/sundheds-og-seksualundervisning-og-familiekundskab](https://www.emu.dk/modul/sundheds-og-seksualundervisning-og-familiekundskab)

## B. Læseplaner og fagmål / Curricula and Subject Objectives

### 3. Fælles Mål — SSF (*Sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab*)

- **Type:** National curriculum objectives / Fælles Mål | **Year:** 2019 (with updates)
- **Publisher:** Undervisningsministeriet / EMU (Danmarks Læringsportal)
- **Description:** The *Fælles Mål* (Common Objectives) for SSF define competence targets and content for each grade cluster (1–3, 4–6, 7–9). Key themes: body and sexuality, health and illness, family and social relationships, reproduction and contraception, diversity and identity. Available with accompanying teaching guides and exemplar materials on the EMU portal.
- **Access (EMU):** [https://www.emu.dk/grundskole/sundheds-og-seksualundervisning-og-familiekundskab](https://www.emu.dk/grundskole/sundheds-og-seksualundervisning-og-familiekundskab)
- **Fælles Mål direct (Undervisningsministeriet):** [https://www.uvm.dk/folkeskolen/fag-timetal-og-overgange/faelles-maal](https://www.uvm.dk/folkeskolen/fag-timetal-og-overgange/faelles-maal)

### 4. Gymnasiereformen 2017 — Læreplaner for stx, htx, hhx (health content)

- **Type:** Upper secondary curriculum | **Year:** 2017 (reform); 2023 (updates)
- **Publisher:** Undervisningsministeriet
- **Description:** Reform of upper secondary education (*Gymnasium*) with new subject curricula. Health content at this level is primarily delivered through Biology (*Biologi*), Social Studies (*Samfundsfag*), and Physical Education (*Idræt*). The reform introduced a stronger emphasis on critical thinking and scientific literacy in health topics.
- **Access:** [https://www.uvm.dk/gymnasiale-uddannelser/fag-og-laereplaner](https://www.uvm.dk/gymnasiale-uddannelser/fag-og-laereplaner)
