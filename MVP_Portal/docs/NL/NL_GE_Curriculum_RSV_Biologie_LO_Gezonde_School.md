# Nederland – Gezondheidsonderwijs: Curriculum Content (VO Biologie + RSV + LO)

## 1. Overview: Transversal Health Education Model

The Netherlands follows a **transversal model** with no single mandatory "Health Education" subject. Key health carriers are:

| Vak (Subject) | Type | Primary health contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Biologie** | Mandatory (HAVO/VWO); varies in VMBO | Human biology; disease; reproduction; sexual health |
| **Lichamelijke Opvoeding (LO)** | Mandatory all levels | Physical health; movement; lifestyle |
| **Relationele en seksuele vorming (RSV)** | Mandatory cross-curricular topic | Sexuality; consent; relationships; LGBTQ+ |
| **Maatschappijleer** | Mandatory in all vo streams | Public health; social determinants; healthcare system |
| **Verzorging / Zorg & Welzijn** | Mostly VMBO | Nutrition; personal health; caregiving |
| **Gezonde School** | Voluntary school programme | Comprehensive whole-school health approach |

## 2. Relationele en Seksuele Vorming (RSV) – Mandatory Cross-Curricular

### 2.1 Wettelijke Basis en Status

**RSV** (Relationele en seksuele vorming) is legally required based on **Kerndoel 43** for voortgezet onderwijs and kerndoel 38 for basisonderwijs (since 2012).

**Implementation freedom**: Schools decide:
- Which subjects deliver RSV
- Which materials and methods to use
- How many hours to allocate
- Within their *schoolidentiteit* (school identity, including religious)

### 2.2 Core Content (SLO Leerplankader RSV)

**SLO** has published a *leerplankader* (curriculum framework) for RSV that most schools use as reference:

**Thema 1: Lichaam en seksualiteit (Body and Sexuality)**
- Puberteitsveranderingen (puberty changes): physical, emotional; individual variation
- Anatomie van het voortplantingsstelsel (anatomy): male and female systems
- Seksualiteit als onderdeel van identiteit en persoonlijkheid (sexuality as identity component)
- Seksuele gezondheid: anticonceptie; SOA's (Seksueel overdraagbare aandoeningen = STIs); HPV-vaccinatie

**Thema 2: Relaties (Relationships)**
- Vriendschap; verliefdheid; liefde; samenzijn; relaties beëindigen
- Communicatie over grenzen, wensen en gevoelens
- Ongelijke machtsverhoudingen in relaties; verleiding; presión van peers
- Online relaties: veilig datingsgedrag; risico's van online contact

**Thema 3: Instemming en grenzen (Consent and Boundaries)**
- **Instemming** (*toestemming*) als kernwaarde – versterkt door Wet seksuele misdrijven 2024
- Grenzen herkennen: eigen grenzen en grenzen van anderen
- "Nee" zeggen en "nee" horen: oefenen
- Seksuele intimidatie (*intimidation*): definities; reacties; aangifte
- Digitaal: niet-vrijwillig verspreiden van naaktfoto's (*sexting*; *wraakporno*) – strafbaar in Nederland

**Thema 4: Seksuele diversiteit en identiteit (Sexual Diversity and Identity)**
- Seksuele oriëntatie: het spectrum; LHBTIQ+ identiteiten
- Genderidentiteit en genderexpressie
- Discriminatie en pesten op basis van seksuele identiteit: herkennen; voorkomen
- **COC Nederland**: LGBTQ+ organisatie met schoolprogramma *Paarse Vrijdag* (Purple Friday)

**Thema 5: Mediawijsheid en seksualiteit (Media literacy and sexuality)**
- Pornografie: realisme vs. fictie; ongelijke machtsverhoudingen vaak afgebeeld
- Reclame en lichaamsbeelden: kritisch analyseren
- Sociale media: invloed op zelfbeeld; *sexting*; deelnemen aan digitale seksuele cultuur

**Thema 6: Seksuele weerbaarheid (Sexual resilience)**
- Zelfkennis en zelfvertrouwen als basis voor grenzen bewaken
- Weerstand bieden aan druk
- Hulp zoeken: naar wie; hoe; Sense.info (Rutgers); Centrum Seksueel Geweld

### 2.3 LHBTIQ+ in Dutch Schools

Kerndoel 43 expliciet noemt **seksuele diversiteit** – dit is juridisch bindend.

**COC Nederland** runs **Paarse Vrijdag** (Purple Friday) – annual school event against LGBTQ+ discrimination; thousands of schools participate.

Aanvullend: Gay-Straight Alliance (GSA) / Genderbewust onderwijs als concept in Amsterdam en progressive scholen.

### 2.4 Consent (Instemming) – 2024 Wet Enhancement

Following the **Wet seksuele misdrijven** (1 July 2024):
- **Rutgers** issued updated school materials emphasising the new legal standard
- GGD/Gezonde School advisors received updated RSV training
- Topic now explicitly addresses the new criminal standard: any non-consensual sexual contact is illegal
- Materials include how to identify and report sexual violence to *Centrum Seksueel Geweld*

## 3. Biologie – Primary Academic Health Carrier

### 3.1 Onderbouw VO (Grades 1–3, Ages 12–15)

All vo tracks include Biology in the onderbouw:

**Health and human body:**
- Organ systems: digestive, respiratory, circulatory, immune, nervous, reproductive
- Disease mechanisms: bacteria, viruses, immune response; vaccination
- Lifestyle and health: diet, physical activity, sleep, stress, substances
- Reproductive biology: male/female anatomy; conception; pregnancy

**In combination with RSV:**
- Sexuality is covered in Biology (biological aspects) AND RSV materials (relational, ethical, identity aspects)

### 3.2 Bovenbouw VO – HAVO/VWO Examenprogramma Biologie

**Domein E: Afweer (Immunity)**
- Innate vs adaptive immunity; antibodies; vaccines
- Allergies; autoimmune diseases
- **AMR** (Antibioticaresistentie): mechanisms; MRSA; multidrug resistance; RIVM national monitoring

**Domein F: Voortplanting (Reproduction & Sexuality)**
- Human reproduction: anatomie; cyclus; bevruchting; zwangerschap; geboorte
- Contraception: methods and effectiveness; Pearl Index
- STIs/SOA's: epidemiology; prevention and treatments
- IVF and reproductive technology: bioethische aspecten
- **Reflectie op seksualiteit op biologisch, medisch, maatschappelijk en persoonlijk vlak** – required explicit in examenprogramma

**Domein G: Ecologie (Ecology)**
- Ecosystems; biodiversity; food chains
- Human impact on environment: pollution; climate change
- **Planetary Health connection**: climate change → disease; deforestation → zoonoses risk; AMR in food chain

## 4. Lichamelijke Opvoeding (PE) – Physical Health

### 4.1 Mandatory for All VO Students

**LO** is mandatory throughout voortgezet onderwijs:
- Wide range of sports and movement activities
- Health knowledge: physical activity effects on body and mind; FITT principle (Frequency, Intensity, Time, Type)
- First aid (EHBO): basic response; CPR awareness
- Physical self-efficacy: developing positive relationship with one's body

### 4.2 Health Knowledge Integration

Modern LO increasingly integrates **gezondheidseducatie**:
- Why exercise matters: cardiovascular, metabolic, mental health benefits
- Sedentary behaviour: risks of too much sitting/screen time
- Sleep and recovery
- Body image and sport: risks of overtraining; eating disorders in youth sports

## 5. Gezonde School – Vrijwillig maar Krachtig Programma

### 5.1 What is Gezonde School?

**Gezonde School** is a voluntary national programme (managed by GGD GHOR since 2017) with a **whole-school approach** to health. Schools voluntarily adopt it and can earn thematic certificates (*vignet*).

**The Four Pillars Model:**
1. **Educatie** – health lessons and learning
2. **Omgeving** – healthy physical/social school environment
3. **Signalering** – identifying students who need support
4. **Beleid** – embedding health in school policy

**Available health themes for certification:**
- Relationele en seksuele vorming
- Bewegen en sport (Physical activity)
- Voeding (Nutrition)
- Roken, alcohol, drugs (Substance use prevention)
- Welbevinden en mentale gezondheid (Well-being and mental health)
- Buitenspelen (Outdoor play)

### 5.2 Lekker naar buiten!

New (2022+) government initiative funding schools for **outdoors and nature-based health activities** (*lekker naar buiten* = "great to go outside"):
- Direct funding to schools for outdoor activities
- One Health/Planetary Health connection: nature contact = health resource
