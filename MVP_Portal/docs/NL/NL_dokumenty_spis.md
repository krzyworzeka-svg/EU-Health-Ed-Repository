## System Overview

The Netherlands has a **decentralised education system** with strong school autonomy. Health education is not a standalone mandatory subject at secondary level but is embedded primarily in **Biology** (*Biologie*) and supported by the **Gezonde School** (Healthy School) model. The **Kerndoelen** (core objectives for primary) and **Examenprogramma's** (examination programmes for secondary) define learning outcomes. A distinctive feature is **verzuiling** (pillarisation) — schools can be public, Catholic, Protestant, or of other denominations, which can affect RSV content. The **GGD** (Municipal Health Service) and **Rutgers** (expertise centre for sexuality) are key support organisations for schools.

## A. Wettelijk kader / Legal Framework

### 1. Wet op het voortgezet onderwijs (WVO) — *Secondary Education Act*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Wet | **Year:** 1963 (latest consolidated version current)
- **Publisher:** Ministerie van Onderwijs, Cultuur en Wetenschap (OCW)
- **Description:** The foundational law governing secondary education in the Netherlands. Establishes the overall framework for curriculum, subject requirements, and school autonomy. Provides the legal basis for the *Kerndoelen* and *Examenprogramma's*.
- **Access (wetten.overheid.nl):** [https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002399/](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002399/)

---

### 2. Kerndoelen Basisonderwijs en Onderbouw VO — health and biology content

- **Type:** Core objectives / Kerndoelen | **Year:** 2006 (primary); 2012 (lower secondary); revision 2022–2025
- **Publisher:** Ministerie van OCW / SLO (National Curriculum Institute)
- **Description:** The *Kerndoelen* define minimum learning objectives for primary and lower secondary education. Health-related objectives are embedded in Biology, Physical Education, and social development goals. An ongoing revision process (2022–2025) is updating the *Kerndoelen* with stronger health competency emphasis, including sexuality education.
- **Access (SLO):** [https://www.slo.nl/thema/meer/kerndoelen/](https://www.slo.nl/thema/meer/kerndoelen/)
- **OCW curriculum revision:** [https://www.curriculum.nu](https://www.curriculum.nu)

## B. Curriculum en programma's / Curricula and Programmes

### 3. Examenprogramma Biologie — *havo/vwo* (most recent)

- **Type:** Examination programme / Examenprogramma | **Year:** Current (most recent update)
- **Publisher:** College voor Toetsen en Examens (CvTE) / OCW
- **Description:** The national examination programme for Biology at *havo* and *vwo* level. Contains the health-related content domains for upper secondary: human physiology, sexual and reproductive health, ecology and environmental health. Biology is the primary subject carrying health content at Dutch secondary level.
- **Access (Examenblad):** [https://www.examenblad.nl/vak/biologie/2024/vwo/](https://www.examenblad.nl/vak/biologie/2024/vwo/)

---

### 4. Gezonde School — *Programma en Vignet* (RIVM/GGD)

- **Type:** National programme / Nationaal programma | **Year:** 2014–present (ongoing)
- **Publisher:** RIVM (National Institute for Public Health and the Environment) / GGD / OCW / VWS
- **Description:** The **Gezonde School** programme is the primary framework for health education in Dutch schools. Schools can earn the *Gezonde School vignet* by implementing structured health interventions across 8 themes: nutrition, physical activity, mental health, sexual health, substance prevention, social skills, digital health, and outdoor/environment. Over 4,000 Dutch schools participate.
- **Access:** [https://www.gezondeschool.nl](https://www.gezondeschool.nl)

---

### 5. Lang Leve de Liefde — RSV Programme (Rutgers / RIVM)

- **Lang Leve de Liefde** (Long Live Love) is the most widely used sexuality education programme in Dutch secondary schools. Developed by Rutgers, evidence-based, and aligned with WHO/BZgA standards. Covers: relationships, sexuality, consent, contraception, STIs, diversity. Used in ~1,500 schools annually.
- **Type:** Evidence-based educational programme | **Year:** Ongoing (regularly updated)
- **Publisher:** Rutgers (expertise centre for sexuality)
- **Access:** [https://www.langlevedeliefde.nl](https://www.langlevedeliefde.nl)
- **Rutgers schools page:** [https://www.rutgers.nl/scholen](https://www.rutgers.nl/scholen)

## C. Gezondheidsdata / Health Data

### 6. Gezondheidsmonitor Jeugd (GGD / CBS, biennial)

- **Type:** National health monitoring / Gezondheidsmonitor | **Year:** Biennial (most recent 2022)
- **Publisher:** CBS (Statistics Netherlands) / GGD GHOR / RIVM
- **Description:** The biennial national youth health monitor, covering all Dutch secondary school students. Data on physical health, mental health, substance use, sexual health, and wellbeing. Key empirical basis for health education policy and Gezonde School programme design.
- **Access (RIVM):** [https://www.rivm.nl/gezondheidsmonitor-jeugd](https://www.rivm.nl/gezondheidsmonitor-jeugd)
