## System Overview

Italy has a **partially centralised** education system managed by the **Ministero dell'Istruzione e del Merito (MIM)**. There is **no standalone health education subject** in Italian schools. Health education is delivered through: (1) **Educazione civica** (Civic Education — mandatory since 2019, includes health as a theme), (2) **Biologia** (Biology — primary carrier for sexual and physical health content), and (3) optional school projects. The **Meloni government** (from 2022) has taken a conservative stance on sexuality education, opposing gender theory and emphasising traditional family values. The **ISS** (Istituto Superiore di Sanità) is the key health data and policy body.

## A. Quadro normativo / Legal Framework

### 1. Legge 20 agosto 2019, n. 92 — *Introduzione dell'insegnamento scolastico dell'educazione civica*

- **Titolo completo / Full title:** Legge 20 agosto 2019, n. 92 — Introduzione dell'insegnamento scolastico dell'educazione civica
- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Legge | **Year:** 2019
- **Publisher:** Parlamento italiano / Gazzetta Ufficiale
- **Description:** Reintroduces Educazione civica as a mandatory subject (min. 33 hours/year) in all Italian schools from primary to upper secondary. One of the three pillars is **sustainable development and environmental education**, which includes health, wellbeing, and One Health themes. The other two pillars cover the Constitution and digital citizenship.
- **Access (normattiva.it):** [https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2019-08-20;92](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2019-08-20;92)

### 2. Linee guida per l'insegnamento dell'educazione civica — MIM 2020 (and 2024 update)

- **Type:** Ministerial guidelines / Linee guida | **Year:** 2020 (base); 2024 (update)
- **Publisher:** Ministero dell'Istruzione (now MIM)
- **Description:** Official guidelines implementing the 2019 Educazione civica law. Include health-related content requirements under the sustainability pillar. The 2024 update by the Meloni government modified some content emphases.
- **Access (MIM):** [https://www.mim.gov.it/educazione-civica](https://www.mim.gov.it/educazione-civica)

## B. Dati sulla salute / Health Data

### 3. HBSC Italia 2022 — Health Behaviour in School-aged Children

- **Type:** Research report | **Year:** 2022 | **Publisher:** ISS (Istituto Superiore di Sanità) / WHO
- **Description:** Italy's HBSC study contribution. Data on health behaviours, wellbeing, substance use, and sexual health of Italian adolescents. Coordinated by ISS. Key reference for health education policy.
