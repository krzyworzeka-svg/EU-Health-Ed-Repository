## System Overview

Finland has one of Europe's most developed health education systems. **Terveystieto** (Health Education) is a **mandatory standalone subject** in the *peruskoulu* (basic school, grades 7–9, ages 13–16) and a mandatory/optional subject in the *lukio* (upper secondary, TE1 mandatory, TE2–TE3 optional). Finland introduced *Terveystieto* as a standalone subject already in 2001 — one of the earliest in Europe. The **Finnish National Core Curriculum (NCC)** defines the objectives and content for all subjects. The **Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL)** provides the key health data. Finland is also notable for its **school health nurse** (*kouluterveydenhoitaja*) model with nurses present in all schools.

## A. Lainsäädäntö / Legal Framework

### 1. Perusopetuslaki 628/1998 — *Basic Education Act*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Laki | **Year:** 1998 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Opetus- ja kulttuuriministeriö (Ministry of Education and Culture)
- **Description:** The foundational law governing Finnish basic education (*perusopetus*). Establishes *Terveystieto* as a mandatory subject in grades 7–9 (added by amendment in 2001). Defines the overall purposes of education including wellbeing, health, and equality.
- **Access (Finlex):** [https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/1998/19980628](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/1998/19980628)

---

### 2. Lukiolaki 714/2018 — *Upper Secondary Education Act*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Laki | **Year:** 2018
- **Publisher:** Opetus- ja kulttuuriministeriö
- **Description:** Governs upper secondary (*lukio*) education in Finland. Establishes the requirement for all students to complete TE1 (*Terveystieto 1*, 2 credits, mandatory) and optionally TE2–TE3. Sets the framework for the NCC 2019 curriculum for upper secondary.
- **Access (Finlex):** [https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/2018/20180714](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/2018/20180714)

## B. Opetussuunnitelmat / National Core Curricula

### 3. Perusopetuksen opetussuunnitelman perusteet 2014 (NCC 2014) — *Terveystieto vuosiluokat 7–9*

- **Täydellinen nimi / Full title:** Perusopetuksen opetussuunnitelman perusteet 2014
- **Type:** National Core Curriculum / Opetussuunnitelma | **Year:** 2014 (in force from 2016)
- **Publisher:** Opetushallitus (Finnish National Agency for Education, EDUFI)
- **Description:** The National Core Curriculum for basic education defines the objectives and core content of *Terveystieto* for grades 7–9. Content covers: health as a resource; physical, mental, social and environmental health; relationships and sexuality; substance prevention; health literacy; navigating the health system. Available in Finnish, Swedish, and English.
- **Access (EDUFI / OPS2016):** [https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/perusopetuksen-opetussuunnitelman-perusteet](https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/perusopetuksen-opetussuunnitelman-perusteet)
- **Direct PDF (Finnish):** [https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/perusopetuksen_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2014.pdf](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/perusopetuksen_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2014.pdf)

---

### 4. Lukion opetussuunnitelman perusteet 2019 (NCC 2019) — *Terveystieto TE1, TE2, TE3*

- **Täydellinen nimi / Full title:** Lukion opetussuunnitelman perusteet 2019
- **Type:** National Core Curriculum | **Year:** 2019 (fully in force from 2021)
- **Publisher:** Opetushallitus (EDUFI)
- **Description:** The NCC for upper secondary education defines three *Terveystieto* courses: **TE1** (mandatory, 2 credits): health and functional capacity, mental health, relationships and sexuality, health information literacy; **TE2** (voluntary, 2 credits): health promotion, society, inequalities; **TE3** (voluntary, 2 credits): deepened topics chosen by student. Assessment is grade-based at TE1.
- **Access (EDUFI):** [https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/lukion-opetussuunnitelman-perusteet](https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/lukion-opetussuunnitelman-perusteet)
- **Direct PDF (Finnish):** [https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/lukion_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2019.pdf](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/lukion_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2019.pdf)

## C. Terveysdata / Health Data

### 5. Kouluterveyskysely — *School Health Survey* (THL, biennial)

- **Type:** National health survey / Kansallinen tutkimus | **Year:** Biennial (most recent 2023)
- **Publisher:** Terveyden ja hyvinvoinnin laitos (THL — Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare)
- **Description:** Finland's biennial *Kouluterveyskysely* (School Health Survey) — the most comprehensive source of data on Finnish children's health, wellbeing, sexual health, substance use, and school experiences. Results directly inform the development of *Terveystieto* and school health policy.
- **Access (THL):** [https://thl.fi/fi/tutkimus-ja-kehittaminen/tutkimukset-ja-hankkeet/kouluterveyskysely](https://thl.fi/fi/tutkimus-ja-kehittaminen/tutkimukset-ja-hankkeet/kouluterveyskysely)

### 6. HBSC Suomi 2022 — Health Behaviour in School-aged Children

- **Type:** Research report | **Year:** 2022 | **Publisher:** THL / WHO
- **Description:** Finland's contribution to the international HBSC study. Coordinated by THL. Provides comparative international data alongside the domestic *Kouluterveyskysely*.
- **Access (THL):** [https://thl.fi/hbsc](https://thl.fi/hbsc)
