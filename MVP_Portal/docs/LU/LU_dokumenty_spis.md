## System Overview

Luxembourg has a **highly centralised and multilingual** education system (Luxembourgish, French, German). Health education is delivered through the **SePAS** (*Service de psychologie et d'orientation scolaires*) school counselling and health support structure, and through dedicated curriculum subjects, most notably **Vie et Société** (Life and Society) and **Biologie** at secondary level. The **MENJE** (Ministère de l'Éducation nationale, de l'Enfance et de la Jeunesse) is the national authority. Luxembourg introduced structured **EVARS** (Education à la Vie Affective, Relationnelle et Sexuelle) sessions from 2023–2024, following the French model. A notable feature is the multilingual complexity — curricula exist in French and German.

## A. Cadre légal / Rechtlicher Rahmen / Legal Framework

### 1. Loi du 25 juin 2004 — *Organisation des lycées*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Loi | **Year:** 2004
- **Publisher:** Gouvernement du Grand-Duché de Luxembourg
- **Description:** Organises Luxembourg's secondary education (*lycée*) system, providing the legal basis for subject requirements including health education components. Establishes the SePAS counselling and health support structure in all lycées.
- **Access (legilux.lu):** [https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/2004/06/25/n2/jo](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/2004/06/25/n2/jo)

---

### 2. Règlement grand-ducal — *Programmes des lycées (MENJE)*

- **Type:** Grand-ducal regulation / Règlement grand-ducal | **Year:** Various (updated regularly)
- **Publisher:** MENJE
- **Description:** The Grand-Ducal regulations define the programmes (syllabi) for all subjects in Luxembourg's lycées, including *Vie et Société* and *Biologie* which carry the main health education content. Programmes are published and updated by MENJE.
- **Access (MENJE programmes):** [https://portal.education.lu/spos/Programmes](https://portal.education.lu/spos/Programmes)

## B. Programmes curriculaires / Curriculum Programmes

### 3. Programme de Vie et Société — *Lycée* (MENJE)

- **Type:** Subject curriculum / Programme | **Year:** Current
- **Publisher:** MENJE
- **Description:** *Vie et Société* (Life and Society) is the primary health and social education subject in Luxembourg's lycées. Content covers: personal development, relationships, sexuality, social responsibility, citizenship, and ethical dimensions of health. The subject carries content equivalent to SPHE (Ireland) or EVARS (France).
- **Access (MENJE portal):** [https://portal.education.lu](https://portal.education.lu)

---

### 4. Programme EVARS — *Sessions d'éducation à la vie affective, relationnelle et sexuelle* (2023–)

- **Type:** Structured programme | **Year:** 2023–2024 (introduction)
- **Publisher:** MENJE / SePAS
- **Description:** Luxembourg introduced structured EVARS sessions in secondary schools from 2023–2024, coordinated by SePAS counsellors. Content follows the French EVARS model: body, emotions, relationships, consent, sexuality, STIs, digital dimension. Sessions are delivered by trained SePAS staff.
- **Access (MENJE):** [https://www.menje.lu](https://www.menje.lu)
- **SePAS:** [https://portal.education.lu/spos/](https://portal.education.lu/spos/)
