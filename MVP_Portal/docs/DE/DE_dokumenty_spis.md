## System Overview

Germany has a **strictly decentralised education system** (*Bildungshoheit der Länder*). Each of the 16 federal states (*Bundesländer*) has its own Ministry of Education and its own curriculum — there is no single national health education programme. The **KMK** (Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education) coordinates through non-binding recommendations. The **BZgA** (Federal Centre for Health Education) provides national teaching materials and campaigns. Health education is delivered as a **cross-curricular theme** (*Querschnittsaufgabe*), primarily through Biology (*Biologie*), Physical Education (*Sport*), Ethics/Religion (*Ethik/Religion*), and home economics subjects. Sex education (*Sexualerziehung*) is mandatory in all 16 Länder based on a 1977 Constitutional Court ruling.

> ⚠️ **Key note:** Documents listed below cover the *federal level* (KMK, BZgA) and selected reference *Länder* (Berlin as progressive model; Bavaria as conservative model). For the full legal text of any specific Land, consult the relevant Ministry of Education website.

## A. Bundesebene — Rechtsrahmen / Federal Level — Legal Framework

### 1. Grundgesetz — Artikel 30 und 70 (*Bildungshoheit der Länder*)

- **Type:** Constitutional provision | **Year:** 1949 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Bundesrepublik Deutschland
- **Description:** The constitutional basis for Germany's educational federalism. Articles 30 and 70 assign education (*Bildung*) as a matter for the *Länder*, not the federal government. This is the reason there is no unified national health education curriculum in Germany.
- **Access:** [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/)

---

### 2. BVerfGE 47, 46 (1977) — *Sexualerziehung in der Schule*

- **Type:** Constitutional Court ruling / Bundesverfassungsgerichtsentscheidung
- **Year:** 1977
- **Publisher:** Bundesverfassungsgericht (BVerfG)
- **Description:** Landmark ruling establishing that schools have an autonomous educational mandate to deliver sex education. Parents cannot fully opt their children out of sex education, though schools must respect religious and ideological sensitivities and present information in a balanced way. This ruling remains the legal foundation for mandatory sex education in all 16 Länder.
- **Access (legal database):** [https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de](https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de)
- **BVerfGE 47, 46 summary:** [https://www.servat.unibe.ch/dfr/bv047046.html](https://www.servat.unibe.ch/dfr/bv047046.html)

## B. KMK — Empfehlungen / KMK Recommendations

### 3. KMK-Empfehlung: *Gesundheitsförderung und Prävention in der Schule* (2012, updated)

- **Titel / Full title:** Empfehlung zur Gesundheitsförderung und Prävention in der Schule
- **Type:** KMK Recommendation / Empfehlung (non-binding)
- **Year:** 2012 (with subsequent updates)
- **Publisher:** Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK)
- **Description:** The key federal-level coordination document for school health promotion. Defines health promotion (*Gesundheitsförderung*) as an integral element of schooling covering: nutrition, physical activity, sex education, addiction prevention, mental health, and hygiene. Status: recommendation only — not legally binding on any Land.
- **Access (KMK):** [https://www.kmk.org/themen/allgemeinbildende-schulen/gesundheitsfoerderung.html](https://www.kmk.org/themen/allgemeinbildende-schulen/gesundheitsfoerderung.html)

## C. BZgA — Bundeszentrale für gesundheitliche Aufklärung

### 4. BZgA-Standards: *Sexualaufklärung in Europa* (WHO/BZgA, 2010/2017)

- **Titel / Full title:** Standards für die Sexualaufklärung in Europa: Rahmenkonzept für politische Entscheidungsträger, Bildungseinrichtungen, Gesundheitsbehörden
- **Type:** Policy framework / Rahmenkonzept
- **Year:** 2010 (German ed.); 2017 (updated)
- **Publisher:** BZgA / WHO Regional Office for Europe
- **Description:** The WHO/BZgA standards for sexuality education — an internationally influential framework developed jointly by the German BZgA and the WHO European office. Provides age-appropriate content standards across all developmental stages. Widely used as a reference point for curricula across Europe, including Germany's own Länder curricula.
- **Direct PDF (BZgA):** [https://www.bzga-whocc.de/fileadmin/user_upload/WHO_BZgA_Standards_deutsch.pdf](https://www.bzga-whocc.de/fileadmin/user_upload/WHO_BZgA_Standards_deutsch.pdf)
- **BZgA page:** [https://www.bzga-whocc.de](https://www.bzga-whocc.de)

### 5. BZgA-Studie: *Jugendsexualität 2021* — Repräsentative Wiederholungsbefragung

- **Type:** Research report / Repräsentative Studie | **Year:** 2022
- **Publisher:** Bundeszentrale für gesundheitliche Aufklärung (BZgA)
- **Description:** BZgA's long-running representative survey (repeated every ~5 years since 1980) on sexual knowledge, attitudes, and behaviour of German youth aged 14–25. Key data source for evaluating the effectiveness of sex education in Germany.

## D. Länder-Ebene / State Level — Selected Reference Documents

### 6. Berlin — *Bildung zur sexuellen Selbstbestimmung* (Senatsverwaltung Berlin)

- **Type:** State curriculum guidance / Handreichung
- **Year:** 2016 (with updates)
- **Publisher:** Senatsverwaltung für Bildung, Jugend und Familie Berlin
- **Description:** Berlin's reference guidance for sex and relationship education — considered the most progressive model in Germany. Explicitly includes LGBTIQ+ identities as normative, consent (*Einvernehmlichkeit*) from primary school, digital literacy (cyberbullying, digital violence), and anti-discrimination. Widely cited in comparative research.
- **Access (Berlin Bildungsserver):** [https://www.berlin.de/sen/bjf/schule/](https://www.berlin.de/sen/bjf/schule/)

---

### 7. Bayern — *LehrplanPLUS: Familien- und Sexualerziehung*

- **Type:** State curriculum / Lehrplan
- **Year:** Current (continuously updated)
- **Publisher:** Bayerisches Staatsministerium für Unterricht und Kultus (StMUK)
- **Description:** Bavaria's curriculum framework for family and sex education — the most prominent conservative model in Germany. Strong emphasis on *Erziehungspartnerschaft* (school-family partnership), biological approach, and LGBTIQ+ framed through "tolerance" rather than normativity. Significant role of the Catholic Church in educational culture.
- **Access (LehrplanPLUS):** [https://www.lehrplanplus.de](https://www.lehrplanplus.de)

## E. Gesundheitsdaten / Health Data

### 8. KiGGS / KIDA — *Gesundheit von Kindern und Jugendlichen in Deutschland*

- **Type:** National health survey / Gesundheitssurvey
- **Year:** KiGGS Wave 2 (2014–2017); KIDA 2022–2023
- **Publisher:** Robert Koch Institut (RKI)
- **Description:** Germany's national health survey of children and adolescents. KiGGS provides comprehensive data on physical health, mental health, and health behaviour of German children (0–17). KIDA is the updated 2022–2023 follow-up study. Key empirical reference for health education policy in Germany.
- **Access (RKI):** [https://www.rki.de/kiggs](https://www.rki.de/kiggs)
- **KIDA:** [https://www.rki.de/kida](https://www.rki.de/kida)
