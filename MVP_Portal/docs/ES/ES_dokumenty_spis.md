## System Overview

Spain has a **decentralised education system**: the national ministry (MEFP) defines *enseñanzas mínimas* (55–65% of curriculum), while the 17 *Comunidades Autónomas* develop the remainder. Health education is integrated **transversally** across Biology and Geology, Physical Education (EPS), and Civic and Ethical Values. Spain also champions the **Escuelas Promotoras de Salud** model, linking human health to environmental health through a One Health lens.

## A. Legislación fundamental / Primary Legislation

### 1. Ley Orgánica 3/2020 — *LOMLOE* ("Ley Celaá")

- **Título completo:** Ley Orgánica 3/2020, de 29 de diciembre, por la que se modifica la Ley Orgánica 2/2006, de 3 de mayo, de Educación (LOE)
- **Type:** Organic law / Ley orgánica | **Year:** 2020 | **BOE ref:** BOE-A-2020-17264
- **Description:** The overarching education law. Establishes health education (including affective-sexual education) as a cross-curricular principle. Mandates equality, non-discrimination, and sustainable development integrated into health content.
- **Access:** [https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/2020/12/29/3](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/2020/12/29/3)

---

### 2. Real Decreto 217/2022 — *Enseñanzas mínimas ESO*

- **Título completo:** Real Decreto 217/2022, de 29 de marzo — ordenación y enseñanzas mínimas de la Educación Secundaria Obligatoria
- **Type:** Royal Decree | **Year:** 2022 | **BOE ref:** BOE-A-2022-4975
- **Description:** Implements LOMLOE at ESO level (ages 12–16). Defines the *perfil de salida* including health competencies; sets health-related *saberes básicos* for Biology, EPS, and Civic Values across 4 years.
- **Access:** [https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2022/03/29/217](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2022/03/29/217)

---

### 3. Real Decreto 243/2022 — *Enseñanzas mínimas Bachillerato*

- **Título completo:** Real Decreto 243/2022, de 5 de abril — ordenación y enseñanzas mínimas del Bachillerato
- **Type:** Royal Decree | **Year:** 2022 | **BOE ref:** BOE-A-2022-5521
- **Description:** Implements LOMLOE at Bachillerato level (ages 16–18). Includes health competency objectives on affective-sexual maturity and conflict resolution. Relevant subjects: Biology/Anatomy and Physiology, Physical Education.
- **Access:** [https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2022/04/05/243](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2022/04/05/243)

---

### 4. Real Decreto 315/2025 — *Alimentación sostenible y saludable*

- **Type:** Royal Decree | **Year:** 2025
- **Publisher:** Ministerio de Educación / Ministerio de Sanidad
- **Description:** Addresses sustainable and healthy nutrition in schools. Directly relevant to One Health / Planetary Health dimensions of health education in Spain.
- **Access:** [https://www.boe.es](https://www.boe.es)

## B. Guías y programas curriculares / Curriculum Guides

### 5. Guía de Escuelas Promotoras de Salud (2023)

- **Type:** Policy guide | **Year:** 2023
- **Publisher:** Ministerio de Sanidad / MEFP
- **Description:** National guide for the Health Promoting Schools model — the primary vehicle for integrating One Health principles in Spanish education. Covers healthy eating, active mobility, affective-sexual education, emotional wellbeing, and environmental sustainability. Links student health to planetary health.
- **Access:** [https://www.sanidad.gob.es/areas/promocionPrevencion/saludJovenes/saludEscolar/home.htm](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/areas/promocionPrevencion/saludJovenes/saludEscolar/home.htm)

---

### 6. Ley Orgánica 10/2022 — *Ley de Libertad Sexual*

- **Título completo:** Ley Orgánica 10/2022, de 6 de septiembre, de garantía integral de la libertad sexual
- **Type:** Organic law | **Year:** 2022
- **Publisher:** BOE-A-2022-14630
- **Description:** The "Only Yes Means Yes" law. Directly affects affective-sexual education content in schools: consent, sexual violence prevention, and LGBTIQ+ rights. Requires schools to implement specific educational programmes aligned with the law.
- **Access:** [https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/2022/09/06/10](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/2022/09/06/10)

## C. Datos e informes / Research Reports and Data

### 7. Encuesta Nacional de Salud de España (ENSE)

- **Type:** National health survey | **Year:** 2020–2023 (latest edition)
- **Publisher:** Ministerio de Sanidad / INE
- **Description:** National health survey providing epidemiological data on Spanish youth health: physical health, mental health, substance use, and lifestyle. Key empirical basis for health education policy under LOMLOE.
- **Access:** [https://www.sanidad.gob.es/estadEstudios/estadisticas/encuestaNacional/home.htm](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/estadEstudios/estadisticas/encuestaNacional/home.htm)
