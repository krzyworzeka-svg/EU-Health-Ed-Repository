## System Overview

Hungary has a **centralised education system** governed by the **Nemzeti alaptanterv (NAT)** — the National Core Curriculum. Health education is delivered primarily through Biology (*Biológia*) and Physical Education (*Testnevelés*) and integrated as a cross-curricular theme. The **2021 Child Protection Act** (*Gyermekvédelmi törvény* amendment) introduced severe restrictions on LGBTQ+-related content in schools, prohibiting materials portraying homosexuality or gender transition as normal for minors — this has profoundly affected health and sexuality education. In 2025, the Court of Justice of the EU (CJEU) ruled this law violated EU fundamental rights. Hungary's health education is thus a major case study in the tension between national policy and EU values.

## A. Jogi keret / Legal Framework

### 1. Nemzeti alaptanterv (NAT 2020) — *Egészségnevelés*

- **Teljes cím / Full title:** A Kormány 110/2012. (VI. 4.) Korm. rendelete a Nemzeti alaptanterv kiadásáról, bevezetéséről és alkalmazásáról — updated by 5/2020. (I. 31.) Korm. rendelet (NAT 2020)
- **Type:** Government decree / Kormányrendelet | **Year:** 2020
- **Publisher:** Magyar Kormány / Belügyminisztérium (Education Department)
- **Description:** Hungary's national core curriculum defining health education objectives across all subjects. Promotes a conservative, family-centred approach to sexuality education. Biology is the primary carrier for health content; Physical Education emphasises physical fitness. The NAT 2020 explicitly frames sexuality education within a heteronormative family model.
- **Access (MK — Magyar Közlöny):** [https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/kerettantervek/2020_nat](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/kerettantervek/2020_nat)

### 2. A Gyermekek védelméről szóló 1997. évi XXXI. törvény módosítása — *2021. évi LXXIX. törvény*

- **Teljes cím / Full title:** Az egyes törvényeknek a pedofil bűnelkövetőkkel szembeni szigorúbb fellépés érdekében szükséges módosításáról szóló 2021. évi LXXIX. törvény (the "Child Protection Act amendment")
- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Törvény | **Year:** 2021
- **Publisher:** Országgyűlés (Hungarian National Assembly)
- **Description:** The controversial 2021 amendment that prohibits content in schools (and media) portraying homosexuality or gender transition as normal for persons under 18. Directly restricts LGBTQ+-inclusive health and sexuality education. Subject of CJEU infringement proceedings — the EU Court of Justice ruled in 2025 that Hungary violated EU fundamental rights law.
- **Access (Magyar Közlöny):** [https://njt.hu/eli/TV/2021/79](https://njt.hu/eli/TV/2021/79)
- **CJEU case C-769/22:** [https://curia.europa.eu](https://curia.europa.eu)
