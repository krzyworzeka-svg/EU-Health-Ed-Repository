## System Overview

Bulgaria has a **centralised education system** managed by the **Министерство на образованието и науката (МОН)**. **Биология и здравно образование** (Biology and Health Education) is a **mandatory integrated subject** throughout Bulgarian secondary education — one of the distinctive features of the Bulgarian system is that health education is explicitly combined with biology, making it a named mandatory subject rather than a purely cross-curricular theme. Health content covers: human biology, reproduction, sexual health, mental health, substance prevention, environmental health. The **НЦОЗА** (National Center for Public Health and Analysis) is the key health data body. Bulgaria participates actively in HBSC.

## A. Правна рамка / Legal Framework

### 1. Закон за предучилищното и училищното образование (ЗПУО) — 2015

- **Пълно наименование / Full title:** Закон за предучилищното и училищното образование
- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Закон | **Year:** 2015 (as amended to 2024)
- **Publisher:** Народно събрание (Bulgarian National Assembly)
- **Description:** The foundational Bulgarian education law. Establishes the curriculum framework and the State Educational Standards (ДОС). Provides the legal basis for *Биология и здравно образование* as a mandatory subject and for health education as a cross-curricular theme.
- **Access (lex.bg):** [https://lex.bg/laws/ldoc/2136700024](https://lex.bg/laws/ldoc/2136700024)

---

### 2. Държавни образователни стандарти (ДОС) — *Биология и здравно образование* (МОН)

- **Type:** State Educational Standards / ДОС | **Year:** 2015–2017 (base); updates 2022–2024
- **Publisher:** МОН (Министерство на образованието и науката)
- **Description:** The State Educational Standards define the curriculum objectives and content for *Биология и здравно образование* across all school levels. Includes mandatory content on sexual health, reproductive health, mental health, and environmental health. Available from the МОН curriculum portal.
- **Access (МОН):** [https://www.mon.bg/bg/100725](https://www.mon.bg/bg/100725)
