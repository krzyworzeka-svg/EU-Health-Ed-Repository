## System Overview

Slovenia has a **centralised curriculum** system managed by the **Ministrstvo za izobraževanje, znanost in šport (MIZS)**. Health education is delivered through multiple pathways: the subject **Vzgoja za zdravje** (Health Education) at primary level, integrated content in **Biology** and **Physical Education (ŠŠK)**, and the mandatory **Vzgoja za zdravo spolnost** / **Odnosi in spolnost** within *Naravoslovje in tehnika* and other subjects. The **OIV** (*Osnove vzgoje za zdravo spolnost* — Basics of Health Sexuality Education) is a subject area that has been at the centre of significant public debate. The **Nacionalni inštitut za javno zdravje (NIJZ)** is the key health data organisation.

## B. Zdravstveni podatki / Health Data

### 3. HBSC Slovenija 2022 — Health Behaviour in School-aged Children

- **Type:** Research report | **Year:** 2022 | **Publisher:** NIJZ / WHO
