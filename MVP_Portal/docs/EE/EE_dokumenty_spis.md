## System Overview

Estonia has a **centralised curriculum** system with school pedagogical autonomy. Health education is delivered primarily through **Inimeseõpetus** (Human Studies / Personal, Social and Health Education) — a mandatory subject covering personal development, social skills, health, and sexuality across all grades. Estonia is internationally recognised for its digital infrastructure in education and its comprehensive sexuality education programme. The **Tervise Arengu Instituut (TAI)** (National Institute for Health Development) is the key health data and resources organisation. Estonia participates actively in the international HBSC study.

## A. Õigusraamistik / Legal Framework

### 1. Põhikooli- ja gümnaasiumiseadus (PGS) — *Basic Schools and Upper Secondary Schools Act*

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Seadus | **Year:** 2010 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Riigikogu (Estonian Parliament)
- **Description:** The foundational law governing Estonian schools. Establishes *Inimeseõpetus* as a mandatory subject. Defines the framework for the national curriculum (*Riiklik õppekava*) and school autonomy. Key legal basis for health and sexuality education in Estonia.
- **Access (Riigi Teataja):** [https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/PGS](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/PGS)

---

### 2. Gümnaasiumi riiklik õppekava (GRÕ) — *National Curriculum for Upper Secondary Schools*

- **Type:** National curriculum | **Year:** 2011 (updated 2014, 2022)
- **Publisher:** Haridus- ja Teadusministeerium (Ministry of Education and Research)
- **Description:** The national curriculum defining objectives and content for Estonian upper secondary education. Includes *Inimeseõpetus* content requirements: relationships, sexuality, reproductive health, mental health, social responsibility. Available with supporting materials from the curriculum website.
- **Access (Oppekava.ee):** [https://oppekava.innove.ee/pohikool-ja-gumnaasium/](https://oppekava.innove.ee/pohikool-ja-gumnaasium/)
- **Riigi Teataja (legal text):** [https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/114022014002](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/114022014002)

## B. Õppekava — Inimeseõpetus / Curriculum

### 3. Inimeseõpetuse ainekava — *põhikool ja gümnaasium*

- **Type:** Subject curriculum / Ainekava | **Year:** Current (within GRÕ/PRÕ)
- **Publisher:** Haridus- ja Teadusministeerium / Innove / HARNO
- **Description:** The specific curriculum for *Inimeseõpetus* across all school levels. Content domains: identity and values; relationships and family; sexual health; mental health and wellbeing; social skills; digital life; substance prevention. One of the most comprehensive integrated health education curricula in Central-Eastern Europe.
- **Access (Oppekava):** [https://oppekava.innove.ee/](https://oppekava.innove.ee/)

## C. Tervisedatan / Health Data

### 4. HBSC Eesti 2022 — Health Behaviour in School-aged Children

- **Type:** Research report | **Year:** 2022 | **Publisher:** TAI (Tervise Arengu Instituut) / WHO
- **Description:** Estonia's contribution to the international HBSC study, coordinated by TAI. Key data on health behaviours, sexual health (Estonia has historically high HIV rates — a key policy driver), mental health, and substance use among Estonian adolescents.
- **Access (TAI):** [https://www.tai.ee/et/tegevused/uuringud/hbsc](https://www.tai.ee/et/tegevused/uuringud/hbsc)

---

### 5. TAI — *Seksuaalhariduse materjalid koolile* (sexual health education resources)

- **Type:** Teaching resources | **Year:** Current | **Publisher:** Tervise Arengu Instituut (TAI)
- **Description:** TAI provides the official sexual health education materials for Estonian schools, including teacher guides, student workbooks, and digital resources. Materials cover the full *Inimeseõpetus* curriculum. Available free of charge.
- **Access:** [https://www.tai.ee/et/terviseinfo/seksuaaltervis/seksuaalharidus-koolides](https://www.tai.ee/et/terviseinfo/seksuaaltervis/seksuaalharidus-koolides)
