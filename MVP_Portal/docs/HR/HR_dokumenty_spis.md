## System Overview

Croatia introduced health education (*Zdravstveni odgoj*) as a mandatory cross-curricular subject in 2012, generating significant public controversy. The current framework is governed by the **Kurikulum 2019** reform, which reorganised health education as a **cross-curricular theme** integrated across Biology (*Biologija*), Physical Education (*Tjelesna i zdravstvena kultura*), and other subjects. A distinctive feature is the **prigovor savjesti** (conscience objection) provision, which allows teachers to opt out of teaching certain health education content (particularly related to sexuality), a unique arrangement in the EU. The **Agencija za odgoj i obrazovanje (AZOO)** and the **Ministarstvo znanosti i obrazovanja (MZO)** are the key authorities.

## A. Pravni okvir / Legal Framework

### 1. Zakon o odgoju i obrazovanju u osnovnoj i srednjoj školi (ZOOOSŠ)

- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Zakon | **Year:** 2008 (as amended)
- **Publisher:** Hrvatski sabor / MZO
- **Description:** The foundational Croatian education law. Provides legal basis for curriculum decisions, cross-curricular themes, and the *prigovor savjesti* provision for teachers in health education. Contains provisions about the role of parents and the Catholic Church's influence in Croatian schools.
- **Access (Zakon.hr):** [https://www.zakon.hr/z/317/Zakon-o-odgoju-i-obrazovanju-u-osnovnoj-i-srednjoj-%C5%A1koli](https://www.zakon.hr/z/317/Zakon-o-odgoju-i-obrazovanju-u-osnovnoj-i-srednjoj-%C5%A1koli)

### 2. Nacionalni kurikulum za osnovnoškolski odgoj i obrazovanje (NK OŠ) — Zdravstveni odgoj

- **Type:** National curriculum / Nacionalni kurikulum | **Year:** 2019 (Kurikulum reform)
- **Publisher:** MZO / AZOO
- **Description:** Croatia's 2019 national curriculum reform restructured health education as a cross-curricular theme. Defines health competencies integrated into Biology, Physical Education, and other subjects. The reform followed controversy around the 2012 *Zdravstveni odgoj* programme and the subsequent *prigovor savjesti* debates.
- **Access (Kurikulum.hr):** [https://kurikulum.gov.hr/](https://kurikulum.gov.hr/)
