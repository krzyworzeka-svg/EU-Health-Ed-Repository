## System Overview

Belgium has a **uniquely complex federal education system** divided into **three Communities**: the **Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles (FWB)** (French-speaking, ~4.5M), the **Vlaamse Gemeenschap** (Flemish/Dutch-speaking, ~6.5M), and the **Deutschsprachige Gemeinschaft** (German-speaking, ~78,000). Each community has full autonomy over education. There is **no single Belgian health education system**.

- **FWB**: Introduced mandatory **EVRAS** (*Éducation à la vie relationnelle, affective et sexuelle*) from September 2023 — 5 hours/year minimum, with a structured progressive programme. The EVRAS framework is jointly supported by *Infor-Drogues*, *Questionsanté*, *FARES*, and other health organisations.
- **Flanders**: Health education is integrated across subjects; the *Eindtermen* (attainment targets) define health competencies. *Sensoa* is the Flemish centre for sexuality and health education.
- **German Community**: Smallest system; follows similar cross-curricular integration approach.

## A. Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles (FWB) — Communauté française

### 1. Décret du 20 juillet 2023 — *EVRAS obligatoire dans les écoles de la FWB*

- **Titre complet:** Décret relatif à l'éducation à la vie relationnelle, affective et sexuelle (EVRAS) dans les établissements scolaires de la Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles
- **Type:** Decree / Décret | **Year:** 2023
- **Publisher:** Parlement de la Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles
- **Description:** Makes EVRAS **mandatory** in all FWB schools from September 2023. Minimum of 5 hours/year per school level. Content covers: body, emotions, relationships, consent, sexuality, diversity, digital dimension. Schools must use trained animators from approved organisations. The most significant health education legislation in recent Belgian history.
- **Access (FWB legislation):** [https://www.gallilex.cfwb.be](https://www.gallilex.cfwb.be)
- **FWB EVRAS page:** [https://www.federation-wallonie-bruxelles.be/evras](https://www.federation-wallonie-bruxelles.be/evras)

---

### 2. Guide EVRAS — *Guide de référence pour les animateurs*

- **Type:** Implementation guide / Guide de référence | **Year:** 2023–2024
- **Publisher:** ONE / CEFO / FWB / Questionsanté
- **Description:** The official EVRAS implementation guide for trained animators working in FWB schools. Defines age-appropriate content, methodology, and safe practice guidelines for each school level. Key operational document for EVRAS delivery.
- **Access (ONE):** [https://www.one.be/index.php?id=evras](https://www.one.be/index.php?id=evras)

## B. Vlaamse Gemeenschap / Flemish Community

### 3. Eindtermen — *Attainment targets including health content* (current edition)

- **Type:** Attainment targets / Eindtermen | **Year:** 2019 (secondary); updated 2023
- **Publisher:** Vlaamse overheid / Departement Onderwijs en Vorming
- **Description:** The Flemish *Eindtermen* define what all students must know and be able to do at each educational level. Health-related competencies are distributed across multiple subject areas (Biology, Physical Education, Social Sciences). The 2019 reform strengthened health competency requirements.
- **Access (Vlaanderen):** [https://onderwijs.vlaanderen.be/eindtermen](https://onderwijs.vlaanderen.be/eindtermen)

---

### 4. Sensoa — *Gezondheid en Seksualiteit: Leermateriaal voor scholen*

- **Type:** Teaching resources / Leermateriaal | **Year:** Current
- **Publisher:** Sensoa (Flemish centre for sexuality and health)
- **Description:** Sensoa is the Flemish reference centre for sexuality education and sexual health. Develops and provides teaching materials for Flemish schools aligned with the *Eindtermen*. Key resources include the *Vlaams Actieplan Seksuele Gezondheid* and school-specific guides for different age groups.
- **Access:** [https://www.sensoa.be/scholen](https://www.sensoa.be/scholen)
