## System Overview

Portugal delivers health education primarily through the **Cidadania e Desenvolvimento** curriculum component, within which *Saúde* (Health) is a **mandatory dimension** that schools must address in at least one school year per educational cycle. The foundational curricular guidance document is the **Referencial de Educação para a Saúde** (DGE/DGS, 2017), organised around 5 global themes. The **Lei n.º 60/2009** mandates a minimum of 12 hours/year of sexuality education at secondary and 3rd cycle level. Since 2018, the **Decreto-Lei n.º 55/2018** gives schools up to 25% curriculum flexibility, enabling schools to develop their own health education projects. The **PNSE** (National School Health Programme) is coordinated by the DGS through the health system.

## A. Legislação fundamental / Primary Legislation

### 1. Lei n.º 60/2009, de 6 de agosto — *Educação Sexual em Meio Escolar*

- **Título completo:** Lei n.º 60/2009, de 6 de agosto, que estabelece o regime de aplicação da educação sexual em meio escolar
- **Type:** Act of Parliament / Lei | **Year:** 2009
- **Publisher:** Assembleia da República / Diário da República
- **Description:** Establishes mandatory sexuality education across all cycles of basic and secondary education. Requires a **minimum of 12 hours/year** at secondary and 3rd cycle levels (6 hours/year at 1st and 2nd cycles). Content must be delivered by qualified teachers, and coordination with health professionals is encouraged. This law is the foundation for sexuality education in Portuguese schools.
- **Direct PDF (Diário da República):** [https://dre.pt/application/file/a/218497](https://dre.pt/application/file/a/218497)

---

### 2. Decreto-Lei n.º 55/2018, de 6 de julho — *Autonomia e Flexibilidade Curricular*

- **Título completo:** Decreto-Lei n.º 55/2018, de 6 de julho — currículo dos ensinos básico e secundário, avaliação e certificação
- **Type:** Decree-Law / Decreto-Lei | **Year:** 2018
- **Publisher:** Ministério da Educação (ME) / Diário da República
- **Description:** Establishes the **Autonomia e Flexibilidade Curricular** framework. Introduces *Cidadania e Desenvolvimento* as a mandatory curriculum area in which *Saúde* is a compulsory dimension. Schools may manage up to 25% of curriculum time for their own projects, including health education. Central to how health education is organised in Portuguese schools since 2018.
- **Access (Diário da República):** [https://dre.pt/application/file/a/115652962](https://dre.pt/application/file/a/115652962)

---

### 3. Despacho n.º 6478/2017 — *Perfil dos Alunos à Saída da Escolaridade Obrigatória (PASEO)*

- **Type:** Ministerial dispatch / Despacho | **Year:** 2017
- **Publisher:** Ministério da Educação
- **Description:** Establishes the **PASEO** — the student competency profile for the end of compulsory schooling. Defines 10 competency areas including **"Bem-Estar, Saúde e Ambiente"** (Wellbeing, Health and Environment). This area explicitly connects human health, lifestyle choices, and environmental sustainability — the One Health dimension of the Portuguese curriculum.
- **Download (DGE):** [https://www.dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/Curriculo/Projeto_Autonomia_e_Flexibilidade/perfil_dos_alunos.pdf](https://www.dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/Curriculo/Projeto_Autonomia_e_Flexibilidade/perfil_dos_alunos.pdf)
- **DGE page:** [https://www.dge.mec.pt/perfil-dos-alunos](https://www.dge.mec.pt/perfil-dos-alunos)

## B. Documentos curriculares / Curriculum Documents

### 4. Referencial de Educação para a Saúde (DGE/DGS, 2017)

- **Título completo:** Referencial de Educação para a Saúde
- **Type:** Curriculum guidance / Referencial (orientador) | **Year:** 2017
- **Publisher:** Direção-Geral da Educação (DGE) / Direção-Geral da Saúde (DGS) / SICAD
- **Description:** The most operationally important document for health education in Portugal. Covers all levels from pre-school to secondary. Structured around **5 global themes**: (1) Mental Health and Violence Prevention; (2) Food Education; (3) Physical Activity; (4) Addictive Behaviours and Dependencies; (5) Affective Relationships and Sexuality Education. Not prescriptive but adopted as standard by nearly all Portuguese schools.
- **Direct PDF download (DGE):** [https://www.dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/Projetos_Curriculares/ReferencialdeSaude.pdf](https://www.dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/Projetos_Curriculares/ReferencialdeSaude.pdf)
- **DGE — EpS page:** [https://www.dge.mec.pt/educacao-para-saude](https://www.dge.mec.pt/educacao-para-saude)

---

### 5. Estratégia Nacional de Educação para a Cidadania (ENEC) — 2017 and 2025

- **Type:** National strategy / Estratégia nacional | **Year:** 2017 (original); 2025 (updated)
- **Publisher:** Ministério da Educação
- **Description:** The ENEC defines the framework for *Cidadania e Desenvolvimento* as a curriculum space. Defines 8 mandatory domains for secondary schools, of which **Saúde** (Health) is one. The 2025 update reinforces health and sexuality as mandatory pillars. Key for understanding how health education is mandated within Portuguese schools.
- **ENEC 2017 (DGE):** [https://www.dge.mec.pt/cidadania-e-desenvolvimento](https://www.dge.mec.pt/cidadania-e-desenvolvimento)
- **ENEC 2025 resolution:** [https://www.portugal.gov.pt](https://www.portugal.gov.pt)

---

### 6. Programa Nacional de Saúde Escolar (PNSE) — Norma DGS 015/2015 (updated)

- **Type:** National programme / Programa nacional | **Year:** 2015 (with updates to 2023)
- **Publisher:** Direção-Geral da Saúde (DGS)
- **Description:** The national programme coordinating health services in Portuguese schools, implemented by the *Unidades de Cuidados na Comunidade* (UCC) of the NHS. Priority areas: mental health, oral health, healthy nutrition, physical activity, environmental health, sexual and reproductive health, substance prevention, vaccination. Complements the educational framework with a health system perspective.
- **Access (DGS):** [https://www.dgs.pt/saude-escolar.aspx](https://www.dgs.pt/saude-escolar.aspx)

## C. Dados e relatórios / Research Reports and Data

### 7. HBSC Portugal 2022 — *Health Behaviour in School-aged Children*

- **Type:** Research report | **Year:** 2022 | **Publisher:** Aventura Social / ULIS / WHO
- **Description:** Portugal's contribution to the international HBSC study, coordinated by the *Aventura Social* team at the University of Lisbon. Key data on physical health, mental health, risk behaviours, and wellbeing of Portuguese adolescents. Empirical basis for health education and PNSE policy.
