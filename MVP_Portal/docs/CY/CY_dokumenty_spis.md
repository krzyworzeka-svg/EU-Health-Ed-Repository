## System Overview

Cyprus has a **centralised education system** managed by the **Υπουργείο Παιδείας, Αθλητισμού και Νεολαίας** (Ministry of Education, Sports and Youth). Health education (*Αγωγή Υγείας*) is delivered as a **cross-curricular theme** integrated into Biology, Physical Education, and other subjects. The 2023 curriculum reform introduced a strengthened holistic approach to health and wellbeing. Cyprus participates in the HBSC study. The system is influenced by the Greek-Cypriot tradition and the significant role of the Orthodox Church, similar to Greece. The **Κέντρο Πρόληψης** (Prevention Centre) supports health education in schools.
